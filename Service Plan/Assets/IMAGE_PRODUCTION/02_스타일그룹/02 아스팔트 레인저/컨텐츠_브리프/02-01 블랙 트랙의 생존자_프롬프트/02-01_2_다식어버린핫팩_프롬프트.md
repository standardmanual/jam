# 02-01 블랙 트랙의 생존자 — 2. 다 식어버린 핫팩

영하의 추위 속에서 생명줄 같았던 온기의 잔해. 이제는 차갑고 딱딱한 돌덩이가 되었습니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 핫팩 패키지가 얼음처럼 굳은 형상)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a spent hand-warmer packet frozen rock-solid, sitting at the exact center of the frame, its pouch rendered in flat weathered-cream fabric color with one dark shading tone along the creases, a thin crust of frost and jagged ice crystals sheeting across its surface rendered as sharp pale steel-blue polygon shapes. Fine cracks web across the frozen packet where it has been squeezed and abandoned. A light dusting of snow surrounds it on the ground, rendered as flat pale shapes. In the mid-ground behind the packet, the jagged black silhouette of an ice-crusted mountain pass lined with a frozen guardrail looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a bleak overcast winter sky roils with a limited charcoal-to-steel-blue tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, with faint drifting snow streaks. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the packet and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: steel blue and weathered cream dominate, with one small deep red accent marking the packet's faded warning label patch. Layered composition: distant winter sky, mid-ground silhouetted frozen pass, close-up frozen hand-warmer in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
