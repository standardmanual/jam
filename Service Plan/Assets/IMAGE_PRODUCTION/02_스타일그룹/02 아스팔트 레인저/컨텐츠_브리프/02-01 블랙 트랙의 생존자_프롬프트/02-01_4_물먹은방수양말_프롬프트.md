# 02-01 블랙 트랙의 생존자 — 4. 물먹은 방수 양말

방수의 한계를 넘어 발가락 사이로 스며든 축축함. 불쾌함마저 훈장으로 여기는 광기가 서려 있습니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 물이 흘러내리는 양말을 신은 발)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a single silhouetted foot planted mid-frame wearing a soaked waterproof sock, rendered as a pure flat black boot-and-ankle silhouette with no outline, streams of water pouring down from the sock's cuff and pooling at the ground rendered as flat pale steel-blue ribbon shapes with sharp highlight linework. Small splashes and droplets are frozen mid-fall around the foot as flat pale polygon shapes. In the mid-ground behind the foot, the jagged black silhouette of a flooded forest trail lined with dripping bare branches looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a heavy rain-soaked sky roils with a limited charcoal-to-steel-blue tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, with faint vertical rain streaks. Bold, consistent black ink outlines are used only on the water shapes and puddle; the foot and boot remain a pure outline-free silhouette. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: steel blue and weathered cream dominate, with one small deep red accent on a worn boot lace tip. Layered composition: distant rain-soaked sky, mid-ground silhouetted flooded trail, close-up soaked foot in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
