# 02-01 블랙 트랙의 생존자 — 9. 진흙투성이 카라비너

무엇이든 매달 수 있는 든든한 고리지만, 지금은 진흙에 덮여 원래 색이 무엇인지조차 알 수 없습니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 진흙속에 묻혀 있는 카라비너)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a sturdy carabiner half-sunk into a thick clump of drying mud at the exact center of the frame, only its gate and a sliver of its spine breaking free of the mud crust, the exposed metal rendered in flat pale steel-blue color with one dark shading tone, the surrounding mud rendered as a lumpy flat weathered dark-umber and charcoal mass with cracked crust linework obscuring the carabiner's original color. A few small pebbles are pressed into the mud around it as flat pale shapes. In the mid-ground behind the mud clump, the jagged black silhouette of a deeply rutted trail pocked with standing puddles looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a heavy overcast sky roils with a limited charcoal-to-steel-blue tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, with faint streaks of drizzle. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the exposed carabiner metal and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: steel blue and weathered cream dominate against the muted dark-brown mud, with one small deep red accent glinting where a worn safety tape once marked the gate. Layered composition: distant overcast sky, mid-ground silhouetted rutted trail, close-up mud-buried carabiner in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
