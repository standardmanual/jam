# 02-02 폭우 속의 질주 — 1. 물먹은 방수 자켓

쏟아지는 장대비 앞에서는 '100% 방수'라는 마케팅 문구도 무용지물이 됨을 증명하는 무거운 천입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 흠뻑 젖어 무거워진 방수 자켓)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a heavy waterproof motorcycle jacket, soaked completely through, hanging limp as if just pulled off a rider's shoulders. The fabric is rendered in flat slate-gray with deep charcoal shading pooling along every seam and fold where water has gathered, and thick flat white streaks of rain run down its surface. A scatter of heavy raindrops frozen mid-fall surrounds the jacket, rendered as simple flat polygon droplets. One small zipper pull catches a vivid deep red accent, the only warm color in the frame. The jacket sits at the exact center of the frame, well clear of the four corners. In the mid-ground behind it, the jagged black silhouette of a rain-lashed highway overpass with dripping guardrails looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a heavy storm sky churns with a limited slate-gray-to-charcoal tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, streaked with driving diagonal rain lines. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the jacket and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: slate gray and white dominate, with one small deep red accent on the zipper pull. Layered composition: distant stormy sky, mid-ground silhouetted overpass, close-up soaked jacket in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
