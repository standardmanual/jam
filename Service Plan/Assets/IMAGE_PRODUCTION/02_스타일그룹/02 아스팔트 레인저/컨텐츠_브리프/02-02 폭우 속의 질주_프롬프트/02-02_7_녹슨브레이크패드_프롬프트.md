# 02-02 폭우 속의 질주 — 7. 녹슨 브레이크 패드

브레이크를 잡을 때마다 칠판 긁는 소리를 내며 주변 사람들의 고막을 테러하는 쇳덩이입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 녹슨 브레이크 디스크와 패드)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a motorcycle brake disc and caliper assembly, its metal surface pitted and streaked with rust from constant rain exposure, rendered in flat slate-gray with patches of rough rust texture built from coarse grain and cross-hatched ink marks. Where the worn pad grips the disc, a few jagged flat white shock-lines radiate outward like a screech frozen in place, and one hot streak of friction along the disc's edge glows a vivid deep red, the only warm color in the frame. Fine rain streaks run down the metal in thin flat white lines. The brake assembly sits at the exact center of the frame, well clear of the four corners. In the mid-ground behind it, the black silhouette of a steep mountain road switching back through a hairpin turn, guardrails glistening, looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a heavy storm sky churns with a limited slate-gray-to-charcoal tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, streaked with driving rain lines. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the disc, caliper, and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: slate gray and white dominate, with one small deep red accent along the friction streak. Layered composition: distant stormy sky, mid-ground silhouetted hairpin road, close-up rusted brake assembly in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
