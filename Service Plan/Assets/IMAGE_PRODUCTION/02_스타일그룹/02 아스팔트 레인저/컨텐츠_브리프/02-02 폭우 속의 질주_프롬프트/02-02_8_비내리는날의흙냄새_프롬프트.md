# 02-02 폭우 속의 질주 — 8. 비 내리는 날의 흙냄새

페트리코어(Petrichor)의 진한 냄새. 폭우 속을 달린 자만이 폐부 깊숙이 들이마실 수 있는 향기입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 빗물 스민 흙바닥에서 피어오르는 김)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a patch of rain-soaked bare earth at the road's shoulder, dark slate-gray soil pitted with raindrop craters and scattered pebbles and roots rendered in flat coarse texture, heavy drops still striking the ground and throwing up tiny flat white splash-crowns frozen mid-impact. Rising from the wet soil, soft curling wisps of pale off-white steam swirl upward, visualizing the sharp scent of petrichor lifting into the storm air. One small cluster of drops catches a vivid deep red glint, reflecting a hazard light somewhere in the storm, the only warm color in the frame. The steaming earth sits at the exact center of the frame, well clear of the four corners. In the mid-ground behind it, the black silhouette of dripping roadside bushes and bent tree branches leaning over a shallow ditch looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a heavy storm sky churns with a limited slate-gray-to-charcoal tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, streaked with driving rain lines. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the soil texture and any solid-form elements, with the rising steam left as pure outline-free flat shapes. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: slate gray and off-white dominate, with one small deep red accent in the reflected puddle. Layered composition: distant stormy sky, mid-ground silhouetted roadside brush, close-up steaming wet earth in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
