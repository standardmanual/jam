# 02-02 폭우 속의 질주 — 9. 꺾인 우산대

자연의 분노 앞에서 인간의 발명품이 얼마나 나약한지 보여주는 처참한 플라스틱 뼈대입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 바람에 부러진 우산 뼈대)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a broken umbrella skeleton, flipped inside-out by the wind, several of its ribs snapped and jutting at wrong angles, the frame rendered in flat weathered slate-gray with one dark shading tone along each bent joint. Torn shreds of pale off-white canopy fabric still cling to a few ribs, flapping as flat wind-blown shapes with sharp torn edges. One snapped rib's exposed metal tip catches a small vivid deep red gleam, the only warm color in the frame. Heavy rain streaks past the broken frame in thin flat white diagonal lines, with a few raindrops frozen mid-fall as flat polygon shapes. The broken umbrella sits at the exact center of the frame, well clear of the four corners. In the mid-ground behind it, the black silhouette of a bent city lamppost with swaying power lines looms over a rain-swept sidewalk, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a heavy storm sky churns with a limited slate-gray-to-charcoal tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, streaked with driving rain lines. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the umbrella frame and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: slate gray and off-white dominate, with one small deep red accent on the snapped rib tip. Layered composition: distant stormy sky, mid-ground silhouetted bent lamppost, close-up broken umbrella in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
