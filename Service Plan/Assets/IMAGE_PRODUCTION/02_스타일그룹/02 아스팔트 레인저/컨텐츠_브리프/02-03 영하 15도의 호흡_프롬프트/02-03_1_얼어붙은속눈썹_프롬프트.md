# 02-03 영하 15도의 호흡 — 1. 얼어붙은 속눈썹

내뱉은 거친 숨결이 그대로 눈가에 얼어붙은 흔적. 깜빡일 때마다 바스락거리는 소리가 납니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 후드 그늘 사이로 드러난 서리 낀 속눈썹)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a narrow sliver of a hood's shadowed opening, framing a single closed eye at the exact middle of the frame, its eyelashes rendered as sharp bright icy-white spikes fanning outward from the lash line, thick with frost. The skin around the eye is left as a flat pale ice-blue silhouette with no distinguishing features, while the brow above and the cheek below dissolve into solid black hood shadow so no identity or expression is legible. A small deep red cord-lock toggle dangles from the hood's drawstring at the edge of the opening, the only saturated warm accent in the frame. In the mid-ground, the jagged black silhouette of a wind-scoured ridge line and leaning frost-stiffened shrubs looms behind the hood, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a bitter arctic sky holds a limited pale ice-blue to white tonal gradient with coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, and faint streaks of blowing snow. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the hood fabric and cord-lock; the eye and lash area remain pure silhouette with no outline. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background reaching into all four corners. Vivid color contrast: ice blue and white dominate, with one small deep red accent on the cord-lock. Layered composition: distant snow sky, mid-ground silhouetted ridge, close-up frosted eye in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
