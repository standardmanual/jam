# 02-03 영하 15도의 호흡 — 3. 돌덩이 같은 핫팩

미친 듯이 흔들어도 열기를 뱉어내지 않고 파업을 선언한, 배신감이 서린 쇳가루 주머니입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 얼어붙어 딱딱해진 손난로 파우치)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a flat rectangular hand-warmer pouch frozen rock-solid, sitting at the exact middle of the frame on a slab of ice, its fabric surface rendered in flat white with one dark ice-blue shading tone, a network of sharp hairline frost cracks spidering across its face like it has given up and turned to stone. A single torn strip of deep red warning tape clings to one corner of the pouch, curling and frost-stiffened, the only saturated warm accent in the frame. In the mid-ground behind the pouch, the jagged black silhouette of a windswept frozen embankment and skeletal frost-laced branches looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a heavy overcast winter sky holds a limited ice-blue to white tonal gradient with coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, and faint streaks of blown snow. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the pouch and the frost cracks. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background reaching into all four corners. Vivid color contrast: ice blue and white dominate, with one small deep red accent on the torn warning tape. Layered composition: distant overcast sky, mid-ground silhouetted embankment, close-up frozen hand-warmer pouch in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
