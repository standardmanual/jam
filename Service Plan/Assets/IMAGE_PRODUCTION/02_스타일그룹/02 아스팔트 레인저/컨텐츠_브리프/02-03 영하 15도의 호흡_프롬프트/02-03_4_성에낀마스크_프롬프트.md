# 02-03 영하 15도의 호흡 — 4. 성에 낀 마스크

콧물과 입김이 뒤엉켜 거대한 빙벽을 이룬 천 조각. 극한의 호흡 곤란을 유발하는 1등 공신입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 두꺼운 성에로 뒤덮인 넥게이터 마스크)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a tubular neck-gaiter mask hanging at the exact middle of the frame from its own drawcord, its fabric rendered in flat white with one dark ice-blue shading tone, the entire mouth-and-nose area encased in a thick jagged crust of frost built up like a miniature ice wall, sharp icy spikes radiating outward from where breath once escaped. A deep red drawcord toggle hangs at the mask's opening, the only saturated warm accent in the frame. In the mid-ground behind the mask, the jagged black silhouette of a frozen handlebar and bicycle frame looms half-obscured in shadow, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a bitter cold sky holds a limited steel-blue to white tonal gradient with coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, and faint streaks of frozen mist. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the mask fabric and toggle; the frost crust is rendered in flat pale tones with sharp hard edges rather than an outline. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background reaching into all four corners. Vivid color contrast: ice blue and white dominate, with one small deep red accent on the drawcord toggle. Layered composition: distant frozen sky, mid-ground silhouetted bicycle frame, close-up frost-crusted mask in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
