# 02-04 진흙탕의 영광 — 3. 나뭇가지 꽂힌 헬멧

수풀을 뚫고 지나가다 강제로 얻게 된 친환경 안테나. 야생의 위장술로 완벽한 아이템입니다.

**negative prompt**

smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**

A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a mountain bike helmet in flat olive-brown with bold black ink outlines defining its vents and shell ridges, a snapped tree branch jammed diagonally through one of the front vents and jutting upward like a crude antenna, a few small mustard-yellow dry leaves still clinging to the twig. The helmet sits at the exact center of the frame, resting at a tilt as if just pulled off after crashing through undergrowth. In the mid-ground behind it, the jagged black silhouette of a dense thicket of whipping branches and bushes looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a dim woodland sky filters through the canopy in a limited olive-brown to dark umber tonal gradient with coarse halftone grain and scattered dot texture like static. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the helmet and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: olive-brown and mustard yellow dominate, with one small deep rust-orange accent on a warning sticker patch on the shell. Layered composition: distant filtered canopy sky, mid-ground silhouetted thicket, close-up branch-pierced helmet in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
