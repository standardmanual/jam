# 02-04 진흙탕의 영광 — 7. 긁힌 프레임 도장

새 자전거에 난 첫 스크래치의 아픔. 하지만 이들은 이를 '영광의 상처'라 부르며 정신 승리합니다.

**negative prompt**

smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**

A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a section of a bicycle down-tube painted in flat mustard-yellow, a single long fresh scratch gouged diagonally across its surface, the scratch rendered as a sharp bright bare-metal line in bold black ink with tiny curled paint-chip flakes lifted along its edge. The scratched tube sits at the exact center of the frame, angled to catch the light along the gouge. In the mid-ground behind it, the jagged black silhouette of a jumbled rock garden and fallen branches looms where the frame must have struck, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a low trailside sky rendered in a limited olive-brown to charcoal tonal gradient with coarse halftone grain and scattered dot texture like static. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the frame tube and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: olive-brown and mustard yellow dominate, with one small deep rust-orange accent glowing along the exposed metal of the scratch itself. Layered composition: distant trailside sky, mid-ground silhouetted rock garden, close-up scratched frame tube in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
