# 02-05 야간 산악 행군 — 2. 부러진 등산 스틱

체중을 온전히 지탱하다 비명을 지르며 꺾여버린 카본 막대. 이제는 땔감으로나 쓸 수 있습니다.

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a carbon trekking pole snapped clean in two at a sharp diagonal angle, the two halves crossed at the exact center of the frame, the shaft rendered in flat weathered indigo-grey with one dark shading tone, the splintered break point rendered as jagged pale fiber strands catching a cold highlight. The pole's rubber handle grip and a loose wrist strap dangle beside it, rendered as flat dark-green shapes. In the mid-ground behind the broken pole, the jagged black silhouette of a steep rocky mountain switchback lined with looming pines looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a moonless night sky roils with a limited indigo-blue-to-charcoal tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, with a faint jagged ridgeline. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the pole shaft and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: deep indigo blue and forest green dominate, with one small amber accent glinting off the splintered break. Layered composition: distant indigo night sky and ridgeline, mid-ground silhouetted rocky switchback, close-up snapped pole in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
