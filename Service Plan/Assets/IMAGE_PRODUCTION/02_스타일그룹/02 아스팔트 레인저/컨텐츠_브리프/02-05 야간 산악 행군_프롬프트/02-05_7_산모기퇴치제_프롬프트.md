# 02-05 야간 산악 행군 — 7. 산모기 퇴치제

독한 향기에도 아랑곳하지 않고 레깅스를 뚫고 침을 꽂는 전투 모기들과의 치열했던 화학전 잔해입니다.

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a small spray bottle of insect repellent standing upright with its cap off, a faint chemical mist puffing from its nozzle, sitting at the exact center of the frame, the bottle rendered in flat forest-green plastic with one dark shading tone, the drifting mist rendered as a few flat pale-cream cloud shapes with fine spray droplets. A cluster of small mosquito silhouettes hover and scatter away from the mist, rendered as tiny pure flat black silhouette shapes with no outline. In the mid-ground behind the bottle, the jagged black silhouette of a dense mountain trailside thicket looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a moonless night sky roils with a limited indigo-blue-to-charcoal tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, with a faint jagged ridgeline. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the bottle and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: deep indigo blue and forest green dominate, with one small glowing amber accent on the bottle's warning label patch. Layered composition: distant indigo night sky, mid-ground silhouetted thicket, close-up spraying repellent bottle in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
