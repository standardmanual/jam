# 02-05 야간 산악 행군 — 8. 비상용 호루라기

길을 잃었을 때 불기 위해 챙겼으나, 다행히 한 번도 입술을 대지 않은 안도의 플라스틱입니다.

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a small plastic emergency whistle hanging from its neck cord, swaying gently and untouched, sitting at the exact center of the frame, the whistle body rendered in flat weathered-amber plastic with one dark shading tone, the cord looping beside it rendered as a flat forest-green braided shape. A faint cool moonlit gleam catches the whistle's rounded surface, rendered as a small pale highlight. In the mid-ground behind the whistle, the jagged black silhouette of a narrow mountain ridge trail looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a clear moonlit night sky roils with a limited indigo-blue-to-charcoal tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, with a pale crescent moon glowing softly. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the whistle and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: deep indigo blue and forest green dominate, with one small glowing amber accent on the whistle's plastic body. Layered composition: distant indigo moonlit sky, mid-ground silhouetted ridge trail, close-up untouched whistle in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
