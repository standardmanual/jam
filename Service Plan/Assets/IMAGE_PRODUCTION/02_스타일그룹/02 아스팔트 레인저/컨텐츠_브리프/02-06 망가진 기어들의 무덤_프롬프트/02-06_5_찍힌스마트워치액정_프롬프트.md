# 02-06 망가진 기어들의 무덤 — 5. 찍힌 스마트워치 액정

바위에 긁혀 거미줄처럼 금이 간 액정. 심박수는 정상이나 수리비 생각에 심장이 요동칩니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 액정 깨진 스마트워치)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a smartwatch face with a spiderweb of sharp cracks radiating across its cracked glass, rendered as bright thin rust-orange linework fracturing over a flat dark-gray screen, the watch body rendered in flat weathered gray with one dark shading tone. The watch sits at the exact center of the frame, strap coiling loosely at its sides. In the mid-ground behind it, the flat black silhouette of a jagged boulder-strewn trail climbs upward, sharp rock edges rendered as pure flat silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a stark midday sky glares with a limited charcoal-to-pale-gray tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the watch body and strap. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: pale gray and steel-gray dominate, with the rust-orange crack lines as the singular searing accent across the shattered screen. Layered composition: distant stark sky, mid-ground silhouetted boulder trail, close-up cracked watch face in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
