# 02-06 망가진 기어들의 무덤 — 6. 찢어진 압박 타이즈

나뭇가지에 걸려 속살을 강제로 드러내게 된, 기능성을 완전히 상실한 쫀쫀한 천 쪼가리입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 찢어진 압박 타이즈)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a torn compression tight, the stretchy fabric rendered in flat weathered gray with one dark shading tone, ripped open by a jagged flat black tear that exposes frayed threads rendered as sharp bright rust-orange linework fraying outward from the gash. The torn fabric sits at the exact center of the frame, caught and stretched as if snagged mid-motion. In the mid-ground behind it, the flat black silhouette of bare whipping tree branches lines a narrow forest trail, rendered as pure flat silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a dense overcast sky presses down with a limited charcoal-to-slate-gray tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the fabric edges. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: slate-gray and weathered-gray dominate, with the rust-orange frayed threads as the singular accent at the tear. Layered composition: distant overcast sky, mid-ground silhouetted branch-lined trail, close-up torn tights in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
