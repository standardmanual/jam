# 02-07 아스팔트 열기 — 2. 끓어오르는 물통

보냉 물통조차 이기지 못한 지열. 한 모금 마시는 순간 식도가 데일 뻔한 미온수 폭탄입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 김이 오르는 보냉 물통)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on an insulated water bottle standing upright with its cap slightly ajar, thick curling wisps of steam pouring out of the opening and coiling upward, rendered as flat pale amber steam shapes with one darker shading tone. The bottle body is flat burnt-amber with a single deep red pressure-warning stripe wrapped around its middle, a few condensation-like heat droplets sliding down its surface rendered as small flat pale shapes. The bottle sits at the exact center of the frame. In the mid-ground behind it, the black silhouette of a sun-scorched running track and low guardrail looms, wavering heat-shimmer lines rippling across it as flat pale horizontal bands, rendered as pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a punishing noon sky burns with a limited amber-to-deep-red tonal gradient radiating from a small flat sun disc, coarse halftone grain and tiny scattered dot texture like heat static throughout. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the bottle and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: amber dominates, with one small deep red accent on the warning stripe. Layered composition: blazing amber sky, mid-ground silhouetted track, close-up steaming bottle in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
