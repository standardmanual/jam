샤워할 때마다 비명을 지르게 만드는 쓰라린 상처. 떼어내고 싶은 충동을 간신히 참아야 합니다.

Bold, consistent black ink outlines defining the scab's cracked plates, with the knee itself left as a pure outline-free silhouette. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: steel gray and white dominate, with one intense crimson accent across the scab. Layered composition: distant hazy sky, mid-ground silhouetted hill and skateboard, close-up scarred knee in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic gore, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues.
