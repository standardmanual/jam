# 02-09 생존 배낭의 밑바닥 — 2. 구겨진 은박 보온 담요

저체온증의 위기에서 바스락거리며 몸을 감쌌던, 가장 얇고 가장 위대한 생명 구명조끼입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 구겨진 비상용 보온 담요)

**negative prompt**

smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**

A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a crumpled emergency thermal blanket, its surface rendered as sharp angular folds and crinkled creases in a warm brassy mustard-gold tone with one darker khaki-brown shading tone deep in the fold shadows, the crinkle lines drawn as bold jagged linework radiating across the sheet. The blanket sits at the exact center of the frame, loosely wrapped around a huddled shape. In the mid-ground, the pure black silhouette of a crouched figure seen from behind is wrapped inside the blanket, knees pulled to chest, only the curved outline of a hunched back and hood visible, rendered with no outline as a flat silhouette. In the far background, a bleak nighttime mountain ridge looms with a limited charcoal-to-khaki-brown tonal gradient sky, a pale mustard moon glowing low, coarse halftone grain and scattered static-like dot texture throughout. Bold, consistent black ink outlines define the blanket's crinkled folds. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: mustard-gold and khaki-brown dominate, with one small deep-red accent glinting on a fold crease. Layered composition: distant night ridge, mid-ground huddled silhouette, close-up crinkled blanket in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
