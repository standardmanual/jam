# 02-09 생존 배낭의 밑바닥 — 6. 비상금 만원 지폐

배낭 가장 깊숙한 곳 지퍼백에 꼬깃꼬깃 숨겨둔, 정 안되면 택시를 부를 수 있다는 최후의 안도감입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 지퍼백 속 꼬깃꼬깃한 지폐 한 장)

**negative prompt**

smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues, currency symbols, readable banknote text

**생성 prompt**

A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a small resealable plastic bag holding one tightly folded, creased banknote, the bill rendered as an abstract flat khaki-brown rectangle with soft crumple-fold lines, no readable text or numerals, just texture and fold shadows, the ziplock bag drawn with bold flat highlight lines along its plastic creases. The bag sits at the exact center of the frame, held between silhouetted fingers pulling it from a half-open pocket, the fingers and pocket flap rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the mid-ground behind it, the dark silhouette of a slouched backpack rests against a guardrail, and beyond it a distant two-lane road curves into the dusk, rendered as flat black silhouette shapes. In the far background, a hazy dusk skyline glows with a limited khaki-to-mustard-brown tonal gradient sky, coarse halftone grain and scattered dot texture like road dust. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the bag and backpack. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: khaki and brown dominate, with one small mustard-yellow accent glowing on a distant streetlight. Layered composition: distant dusk road and skyline, mid-ground backpack silhouette, close-up folded bill in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, signatures, currency symbols or readable banknote details, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
