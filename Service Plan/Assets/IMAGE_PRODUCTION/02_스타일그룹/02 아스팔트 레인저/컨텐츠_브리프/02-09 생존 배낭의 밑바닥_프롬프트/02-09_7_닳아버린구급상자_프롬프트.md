# 02-09 생존 배낭의 밑바닥 — 7. 닳아버린 구급상자

대일밴드와 연고는 모두 사라지고 정체 모를 알약만 굴러다니는 생존 배낭 속 빨간 파우치입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 낡고 해진 응급 파우치와 흩어진 알약)

**negative prompt**

smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**

A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a worn, frayed first-aid pouch lying open, its fabric a faded deep-red canvas with visible fraying edges and a broken zipper pull, rendered in flat color with one darker maroon-brown shading tone in the fabric folds. A handful of unmarked round and oval pills spill out beside it, drawn as small flat khaki and mustard-tan shapes with a single dark outline each. The pouch sits at the exact center of the frame. In the mid-ground behind it, the black silhouette of a slumped backpack leans against a jagged boulder, its straps hanging loose, other scattered gear pieces rendered as small flat black silhouette shapes nearby. In the far background, a rugged mountain pass rolls beneath a limited khaki-to-brown tonal gradient dusk sky with coarse halftone grain and scattered dot texture like fine grit. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the pouch and boulder forms. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: khaki and brown dominate, with the frayed pouch itself as the vivid deep-red focal accent. Layered composition: distant mountain pass, mid-ground backpack and scattered gear silhouettes, close-up open pouch in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
