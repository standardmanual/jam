# 02-09 생존 배낭의 밑바닥 — 8. 바스라진 육포

형태를 잃고 가루가 되어버렸지만 짭짤한 고기 맛으로 엔돌핀을 돌게 한 훌륭한 비상식량입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 부스러기가 된 육포 봉지)

**negative prompt**

smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**

A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a flattened, empty jerky wrapper with a scatter of crumbled dark-brown meat fragments and fine dust spilling out of its torn corner, the wrapper rendered as a flat crinkled khaki-tan shape with one darker umber shading tone along its creases, the jerky crumbs drawn as small irregular flat brown polygon shapes with bold ink outlines. The wrapper sits at the exact center of the frame. A silhouetted hand pinches a few crumbs toward an unseen mouth just at the frame's edge of center, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the mid-ground behind it, the black silhouette of a weary hiker's profile tilts back mid-chew, jaw and hood outline visible only as solid shadow. In the far background, a sun-baked mountain ridge stretches beneath a limited khaki-to-mustard-brown tonal gradient sky with coarse halftone grain and scattered dot texture like drifting dust. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the wrapper and crumb shapes. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: khaki and brown dominate, with one small mustard-yellow accent highlighting a sunlit crumb. Layered composition: distant sun-baked ridge, mid-ground silhouetted profile, close-up crumbled jerky wrapper in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
