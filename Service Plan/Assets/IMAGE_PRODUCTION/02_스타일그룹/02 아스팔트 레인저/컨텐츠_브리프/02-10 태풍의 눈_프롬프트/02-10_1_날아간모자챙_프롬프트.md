# 02-10 태풍의 눈 — 1. 날아간 모자 챙

강풍에 속수무책으로 뜯겨 날아가버려, 빗속에서 시야 확보를 포기하게 만든 모자의 잔해입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 강풍에 찢겨 날아가는 모자챙)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a torn cap brim ripped clean off a cap, tumbling mid-air with a few loose stitching threads trailing behind it, the brim rendered in flat weathered teal with one dark shading tone along its curved edge. A handful of diagonal rain streaks are frozen around the brim as short flat white-gray slashes cutting across the frame. The brim sits at the exact center of the composition. In the mid-ground behind it, the jagged black silhouette of a gusting roadside — bent street signs and whipping utility wires — looms as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a churning typhoon sky roils with a limited charcoal-to-teal tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the cap brim and any solid-form elements; the rain streaks and wind-torn stitching remain outline-free flat shapes. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: dusty teal and slate gray dominate, with one small acid-yellow accent glowing on a reflective strip inside the brim. Layered composition: distant storm sky, mid-ground silhouetted gusting street, close-up torn brim in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
