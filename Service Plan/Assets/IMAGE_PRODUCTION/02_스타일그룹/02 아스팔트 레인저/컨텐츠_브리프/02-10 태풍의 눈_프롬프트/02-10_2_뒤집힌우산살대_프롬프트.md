# 02-10 태풍의 눈 — 2. 뒤집힌 우산 살대

비바람을 막으려 펼쳤으나 1초 만에 뒤집어져 위성 안테나가 되어버린 처참한 흉물입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 강풍에 뒤집힌 우산)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on an umbrella blown completely inside-out, its ribs bent backward into a dish shape and its canopy fabric flapping violently, the bent metal ribs rendered in flat gunmeal gray with one dark shading tone and the torn canopy panels in flat dusty teal. The umbrella sits at the exact center of the frame, one rib snapped and jutting outward with a sharp bright fracture line. A scatter of heavy raindrops falls around it as flat pale polygon shapes. In the mid-ground behind the umbrella, the jagged black silhouette of a storm-lashed street corner with a bent lamppost looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a heavy typhoon sky roils with a limited charcoal-to-teal tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, with faint streaks of driving rain. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the umbrella ribs and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: slate gray and dusty teal dominate, with one small acid-yellow accent on the umbrella's snapped tip cap. Layered composition: distant stormy sky, mid-ground silhouetted street corner, close-up inverted umbrella in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
