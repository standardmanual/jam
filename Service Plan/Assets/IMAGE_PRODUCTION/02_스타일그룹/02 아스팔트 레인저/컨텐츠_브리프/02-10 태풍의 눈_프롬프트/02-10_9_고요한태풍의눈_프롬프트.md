# 02-10 태풍의 눈 — 9. 고요한 태풍의 눈

폭풍우가 쏟아지다 거짓말처럼 멈춘 찰나의 순간. 하늘이 열린 듯한 기괴한 평화로움입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 폭풍이 멈춘 태풍의 눈 — 하늘 자체가 근경이 되는 장면)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on an eerie circular gap torn open in the middle of a typhoon's wall of clouds, the eye of the storm, rendered as a perfectly still, pale teal-gray patch of open sky ringed by a thick wall of churning dark cloud that curls inward toward the center in bold flat swirling bands. The calm eye sits at the exact center of the frame, its stillness rendered with almost no gradient — just one flat pale tone — in stark contrast to the surrounding turbulence. A single distant bird glides as a small flat black silhouette near the edge of the calm patch. The towering cloud wall around it is rendered in a limited charcoal-to-teal tonal gradient with coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, its inner curling bands picked out with bold black ink linework. Below, the jagged black silhouette of a flooded, debris-strewn road stretches to the frame's edge, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: pale teal and slate gray dominate the calm eye and cloud wall, with one small acid-yellow accent as a thin sliver of true sunlight breaking through the very center of the opening. Layered composition: towering cloud-wall sky, mid-ground silhouetted flooded road, the calm eye itself as the close-up centerpiece — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
