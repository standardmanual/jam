Do

  • Keep the black canvas (--color-bg) — depth comes from borders and grey card surfaces, not shadows.
  • Use the red-orange primary (--color-primary) as the one dominant accent; brown is secondary only.
  • Reserve full-pill radius for buttons and tags; keep cards and inputs at the moderate 8–10px scale.
  • Use weight 600 + tight tracking only on H1/H2; keep body copy at weight 400, normal tracking.
  • Build spacing from the 4px base scale (4/8/12/16/24/32/48/64).

Don't

  • Don't introduce colors outside the palette in tokens/colors.css without documenting why (see the amber/mythic-pink precedent in readme.md).
  • Don't switch any surface to a light/white background — this system is dark-only.
  • Don't apply the bold-display weight/tracking rules to running text — they're for hero numerals only.
  • Don't mix pill radius onto cards or inputs — it reads as a button and steals the buttons' visual signature.
  • Don't add box-shadows — elevation was intentionally removed when the system went black-background.
