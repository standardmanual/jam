# 02-01 블랙 트랙의 생존자 — 1. 녹슨 체인 조각

극한의 악천후를 뚫고 달린 대가. 기름칠할 시간조차 사치였던 치열한 생존의 증거입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 오토바이크의 녹이 슨 굵은 체인)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a thick, heavily rusted motorcycle drive chain, coiled and looped at the exact center of the frame, individual links rendered in flat weathered-cream metal color with one dark shading tone along the link edges, deep rust patches spreading across several links rendered as saturated deep-red flaking corrosion texture. A small discarded tube of dried-up chain lubricant lies beside the coil, rendered as a flat pale shape. In the mid-ground behind the chain, the jagged black silhouette of a storm-lashed asphalt road curving past a battered guardrail looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a heavy rain-laden sky roils with a limited charcoal-to-steel-blue tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, with faint streaks of driving rain. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the chain links and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: steel blue and weathered cream dominate, with one small deep red accent glowing on the worst rust patch. Layered composition: distant rain-heavy sky, mid-ground silhouetted road and guardrail, close-up rusted chain in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
