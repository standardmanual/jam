# 02-01 블랙 트랙의 생존자 — 3. 진흙 묻은 고어텍스 조각

수십만 원짜리 기능성 원단도 대자연의 진흙팩 앞에서는 그저 평등한 헝겊 쪼가리일 뿐임을 증명합니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 진흙이 굳어 붙은 어두운색 작업복 바지)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on the lower leg of a dark technical work pant, caked thick with dried mud from knee to cuff, sitting at the exact center of the frame, the fabric rendered in flat deep steel-blue-grey with one dark brown-tinged shading tone in the folds, the dried mud crust rendered as cracked flat weathered-cream and dark umber patches with fine crackle-pattern linework. A torn fabric flap hangs loose at the cuff, edges frayed. In the mid-ground behind the leg, the jagged black silhouette of a rutted, waterlogged mountain trail cutting through mud looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a heavy overcast sky roils with a limited charcoal-to-steel-blue tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, with faint streaks of drizzle. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the fabric folds and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: steel blue and weathered cream dominate with a muted dark-brown undertone in the mud, and one small deep red accent marking a torn reflective safety strip. Layered composition: distant overcast sky, mid-ground silhouetted muddy trail, close-up mud-caked pant leg in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
