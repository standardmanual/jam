# 02-01 블랙 트랙의 생존자 — 5. 얼어붙은 물통

수분 보충을 위해 깨물었다가 이빨이 나갈 뻔한 위기를 넘긴 흔적. 물이 아닌 빙수가 들어있습니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 꽁꽁 얼은 2차 세계대전 시대의 보병 수통)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a battered World War Two-era infantry canteen frozen solid, standing upright at the exact center of the frame, its body rendered in flat pale steel-blue metal color with one dark shading tone along the dents, a thick bulge of jagged ice cracking the spout open where the frozen water expanded, the ice rendered as sharp bright faceted polygon shapes in near-white and pale steel-blue. A dusting of frost clings to the canvas carrying strap coiled beside it, rendered as flat weathered-cream fabric. In the mid-ground behind the canteen, the jagged black silhouette of a wind-scoured snow ridge looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a pale, bitterly cold sky roils with a limited white-to-steel-blue tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, with faint blowing snow streaks. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the canteen body and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: icy pale steel-blue and weathered cream dominate this coldest, whitest piece of the set, with one small deep red accent on a frayed strap buckle. Layered composition: distant frigid sky, mid-ground silhouetted snow ridge, close-up frozen canteen in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
