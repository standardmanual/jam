# 02-01 블랙 트랙의 생존자 — 6. 흠집 난 고글 렌즈

쏟아지는 우박과 나뭇가지를 온몸으로 막아낸 시야의 수호자. 영광의 상처가 선명하게 남아있습니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 레트로 바이크 고글)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a pair of retro motorcycle goggles with a battered, scratched lens, the scratches and hairline cracks rendered as sharp bright linework across the lens surface like badges of honor from surviving hail and whipping tree branches. The goggles sit at the exact center of the frame, the frame rendered in flat weathered-cream color with one dark shading tone, the lens glass in pale steel-blue with a faint limited reflective gradient. A few small hailstones are frozen mid-fall around the goggles, rendered as flat pale polygon shapes. In the mid-ground behind the goggles, the jagged black silhouette of a storm-battered mountain road lined with bare whipping tree branches looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a heavy storm sky roils with a limited charcoal-to-steel-blue tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, with faint streaks of falling hail. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the goggles and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: steel blue and weathered cream dominate, with one small deep red accent highlighting a crack on the lens. Layered composition: distant stormy sky, mid-ground silhouetted mountain road, close-up scratched goggles in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
