# 02-01 블랙 트랙의 생존자 — 7. 수명이 다한 펑크 패치

인적 없는 산속에서 타이어가 터졌을 때, 떨리는 손으로 꾹꾹 눌러 붙였던 생존 본능의 결실입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 펑크 리페어 킷을 끈으로 묶어서 정리한 모습)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a small puncture-repair kit bundle — a flat patch tin, a stub of rubber cement, and a scrap of sandpaper — all cinched tightly together with weathered cord in a rough knot, sitting at the exact center of the frame, the tin rendered in flat weathered-cream metal color with one dark shading tone, the cord in a duller steel-blue-grey fiber texture with frayed ends. A single spent rubber patch, half-used, lies unrolled beside the bundle. In the mid-ground behind the kit, the jagged black silhouette of an empty, winding mountain road with no other travelers in sight looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a heavy dusk sky roils with a limited charcoal-to-steel-blue tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, with faint drifting mist streaks. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the tin, cord knot, and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: steel blue and weathered cream dominate, with one small deep red accent on the tin's worn rubber cement cap. Layered composition: distant dusk sky, mid-ground silhouetted empty mountain road, close-up bundled repair kit in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
