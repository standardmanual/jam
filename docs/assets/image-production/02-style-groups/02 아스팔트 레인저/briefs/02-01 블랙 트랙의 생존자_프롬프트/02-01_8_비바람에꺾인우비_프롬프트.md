# 02-01 블랙 트랙의 생존자 — 8. 비바람에 꺾인 우비

태풍과 맞서 싸우다 장렬하게 찢어져 나간 얇은 비닐. 바람의 저항을 온몸으로 느낀 잔해입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 바람에 휘날리는 판초 우의)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a thin plastic poncho caught mid-whip in violent wind, its hood and hem torn and flapping in sharp angular folds at the exact center of the frame, the plastic rendered in flat weathered-cream color with one dark shading tone tracing the wind-bent creases, a long jagged tear splitting one side of the poncho with frayed plastic edges rendered as sharp bright linework. A few loose plastic scraps tumble through the air around it as flat pale shapes. In the mid-ground behind the poncho, the jagged black silhouette of wind-bent trees thrashing beside an empty roadside looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a violent typhoon sky roils with a limited charcoal-to-steel-blue tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, with sweeping diagonal streaks of driving rain. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the poncho's torn edges and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: steel blue and weathered cream dominate, with one small deep red accent on a snapped drawstring toggle. Layered composition: distant typhoon sky, mid-ground silhouetted wind-bent trees, close-up torn flapping poncho in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
