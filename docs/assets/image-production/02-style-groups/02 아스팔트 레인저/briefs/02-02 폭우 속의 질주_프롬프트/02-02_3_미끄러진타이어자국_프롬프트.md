# 02-02 폭우 속의 질주 — 3. 미끄러진 타이어 자국

젖은 노면에서 아찔하게 미끄러지며 간신히 중심을 잡은, 심장이 철렁했던 순간의 탁본입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 젖은 노면 위 미끄러진 타이어 자국)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a single curved skid mark torn across wet asphalt, the tire tread rendered as a bold flat black print with jagged edges where rubber tore against the slick surface, water splashing away from the curve in flat white polygon arcs frozen mid-motion. The wet asphalt itself is flat slate-gray with a faint dark shading tone pooling in the low points, mirroring the churning sky above. A single thin stream of water cuts through the skid mark and catches a vivid deep red glint, as if reflecting a distant taillight, the only warm color in the frame. The skid mark sits at the exact center of the frame, well clear of the four corners. In the mid-ground behind it, the black silhouette of a rain-swept intersection with a leaning traffic light pole and faded crosswalk stripes looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a heavy storm sky churns with a limited slate-gray-to-charcoal tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, streaked with driving rain lines. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the skid mark and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: slate gray and white dominate, with one small deep red accent in the reflected water. Layered composition: distant stormy sky, mid-ground silhouetted intersection, close-up skid mark in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
