# 02-02 폭우 속의 질주 — 5. 젖은 스마트폰 케이스

방수폰이라 믿었지만 스피커에서 먹먹한 소리가 나기 시작할 때 찾아오는 공포가 묻어있습니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 물이 스며든 스마트폰 케이스)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a phone in a rugged case, its screen gone dark and dead, water beading across its flat slate-gray surface in scattered polygon droplets. Along the lower edge, tiny flat white bubbles push out from the speaker grille holes, rendered as small round shapes escaping the ports — the visible proof that water has crept inside. One small charging-port indicator light still flickers a faint deep red, the only warm color in the frame, stubbornly glowing before it too fails. The phone sits at the exact center of the frame, well clear of the four corners. In the mid-ground behind it, the black silhouette of a dripping convenience-store awning with a parked motorcycle pulled beneath it looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a heavy storm sky churns with a limited slate-gray-to-charcoal tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, streaked with driving rain lines. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the phone case and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: slate gray and white dominate, with one small deep red accent on the failing indicator light. Layered composition: distant stormy sky, mid-ground silhouetted awning, close-up waterlogged phone in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
