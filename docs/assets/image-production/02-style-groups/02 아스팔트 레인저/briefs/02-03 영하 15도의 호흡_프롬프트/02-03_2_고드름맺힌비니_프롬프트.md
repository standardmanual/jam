# 02-03 영하 15도의 호흡 — 2. 고드름 맺힌 비니

머리에서 뿜어져 나온 열기가 찬 공기를 만나 맺힌 영광의 얼음 기둥. 보는 것만으로도 두통이 옵니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 고드름이 매달린 니트 비니)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a ribbed knit beanie sitting at the exact middle of the frame, its flat weathered white-and-ice-blue knit texture rendered with one dark shading tone along the ribbing, a thick row of jagged icicles hanging frozen from its brim like a crown of daggers. A faint wisp of pale steam rises from the crown of the beanie, rendered as thin flat curling shapes, showing heat still fighting the cold. A single deep red woven pull-tab loop sits stitched at the back seam of the beanie, the only saturated warm accent in the frame. In the mid-ground behind the beanie, the jagged black silhouette of a frozen mountain pass with bare iced-over branches looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a heavy winter sky holds a limited white-to-steel-blue tonal gradient with coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, and faint streaks of drifting snow. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the beanie, icicles, and pull-tab. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background reaching into all four corners. Vivid color contrast: ice blue and white dominate, with one small deep red accent on the pull-tab. Layered composition: distant snow sky, mid-ground silhouetted frozen pass, close-up icicle-crowned beanie in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
