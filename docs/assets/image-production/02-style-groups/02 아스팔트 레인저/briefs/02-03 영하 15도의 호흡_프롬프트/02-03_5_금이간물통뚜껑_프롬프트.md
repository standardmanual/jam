# 02-03 영하 15도의 호흡 — 5. 금이 간 물통 뚜껑

얼어붙은 뚜껑을 억지로 열려다 그만 파괴해 버린, 갈증과 괴력이 낳은 겨울철 참사입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 산산이 금 간 물통 뚜껑과 병목)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on the neck of a frozen water bottle at the exact middle of the frame, its screw cap wrenched loose and split into jagged shards still clinging to the threads, deep hairline cracks rendered as sharp bright linework radiating across the cap like a shattered windshield. The bottle body below is rendered in flat pale ice-blue with one dark shading tone, sheathed in a thin crust of frost. A deep red rubber gasket ring is exposed inside the broken cap, the only saturated warm accent in the frame. A few small ice shards are frozen mid-scatter around the cap, rendered as flat pale polygon shapes. In the mid-ground behind the bottle, the jagged black silhouette of a frost-covered trail signpost and bare branches looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a heavy winter sky holds a limited charcoal-to-ice-blue tonal gradient with coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, and faint streaks of drifting snow. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the bottle, cap, and gasket. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background reaching into all four corners. Vivid color contrast: ice blue and white dominate, with one small deep red accent on the gasket ring. Layered composition: distant winter sky, mid-ground silhouetted signpost, close-up shattered bottle cap in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
