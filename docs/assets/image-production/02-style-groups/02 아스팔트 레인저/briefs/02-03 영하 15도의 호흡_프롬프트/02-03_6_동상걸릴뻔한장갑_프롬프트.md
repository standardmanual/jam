# 02-03 영하 15도의 호흡 — 6. 동상 걸릴 뻔한 장갑

손가락 끝의 감각이 사라지는 공포 속에서 핸들바를 부여잡았던 땀과 눈물의 결정체입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 핸들바를 움켜쥔 장갑 낀 손)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a pair of thick gloved hands gripping a handlebar grip at the exact middle of the frame, the gloves rendered as flat white-and-ice-blue silhouette shapes with one dark shading tone along the knuckles, fine frost crystals dusting the fingertips, the grip tightened hard enough that the knuckle seams strain. A thin deep red reflective strip runs along the back of one glove, the only saturated warm accent in the frame. Below the hands, the handlebar grip fades into pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the mid-ground behind the hands, the jagged black silhouette of a frozen mountain road curving into darkness looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a bitter cold sky holds a limited steel-blue to white tonal gradient with coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, and faint streaks of blowing snow. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the gloves and reflective strip. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background reaching into all four corners. Vivid color contrast: ice blue and white dominate, with one small deep red accent on the reflective strip. Layered composition: distant cold sky, mid-ground silhouetted mountain road, close-up gripping gloves in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
