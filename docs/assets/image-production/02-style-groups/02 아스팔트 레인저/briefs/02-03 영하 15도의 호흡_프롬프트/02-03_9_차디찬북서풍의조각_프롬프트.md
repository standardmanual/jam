# 02-03 영하 15도의 호흡 — 9. 차디찬 북서풍의 조각

살을 에는 듯한 칼바람을 병에 담았습니다. 뚜껑을 열면 뺨을 찌르는 듯한 한기가 퍼집니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 칼바람을 가둔 유리병)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a small glass bottle at the exact middle of the frame, sealed with a stopper, its interior swirling with a captured cyclone of biting wind rendered as sharp jagged ribbons of pale ice-blue and white cutting across one another like frozen blades. A deep red wax seal caps the stopper, the only saturated warm accent in the frame, with a single thin crack of escaping frost mist curling up from beneath it, rendered as a flat pale curling shape. The bottle glass itself is rendered in flat pale steel-blue with one dark shading tone along its curved edge, no glossy highlights. In the mid-ground behind the bottle, the jagged black silhouette of a wind-bent lone tree and rocky outcrop looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a violent winter sky holds a limited charcoal-to-ice-blue tonal gradient with coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, and sharp streaks suggesting a howling gale. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the bottle, stopper, and wind ribbons. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background reaching into all four corners. Vivid color contrast: ice blue and white dominate, with one small deep red accent on the wax seal. Layered composition: distant violent sky, mid-ground silhouetted wind-bent tree, close-up wind-filled bottle in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
