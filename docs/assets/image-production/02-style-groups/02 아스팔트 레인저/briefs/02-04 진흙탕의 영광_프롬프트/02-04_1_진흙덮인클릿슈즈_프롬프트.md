# 02-04 진흙탕의 영광 — 1. 진흙 덮인 클릿 슈즈

페달에 끼워지지 않아 탕탕 치며 분노를 표출하게 만든 덩어리. 신발의 원형을 잃었습니다.

**negative prompt**

smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**

A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a single clipless cycling shoe, its sole and cleat completely packed with thick clumped mud, the original tread pattern lost beneath uneven caked lumps rendered as flat olive-brown clustered shapes with darker umber shading lines at the clump edges. The shoe sits at the exact center of the frame, tilted mid-strike as if being banged furiously against a pedal, with a few flat mustard-yellow mud splatters flung outward from the point of impact, frozen mid-air. The shoe's upper is a weathered mustard-yellow fabric with bold black ink outlines defining its panels and laces. In the mid-ground behind the shoe, the jagged black silhouette of a rutted, root-choked forest trail looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, an overcast sky rendered in a limited olive-brown to dark umber tonal gradient with coarse halftone grain and scattered dot texture like static. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the shoe and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: olive-brown and mustard yellow dominate, with one small deep rust-orange accent on a scuffed buckle. Layered composition: distant overcast sky, mid-ground silhouetted muddy trail, close-up mud-caked shoe in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
