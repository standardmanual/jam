# 02-04 진흙탕의 영광 — 2. 풀잎 엉킨 스프라켓

오프로드를 거칠게 누빈 대가로 구동계에 샐러드가 차려진 기괴하고도 자연 친화적인 풍경입니다.

**negative prompt**

smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**

A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a bicycle rear cassette and derailleur, its stacked sprocket gears rendered in flat mustard-yellow metal with bold black ink outlines on every gear tooth. Long blades of grass, torn leaves, and thin vine tendrils are wound tightly through the gear teeth and chain links, rendered as flat olive-brown and deep-green foliage shapes clinging to the metal like an accidental salad. A few loose leaf fragments float just below the cassette, frozen mid-fall. The sprocket sits at the exact center of the frame. In the mid-ground behind it, the jagged black silhouette of an overgrown off-road trail cutting through tall wild grass looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a heavy dusk sky rendered in a limited olive-brown to charcoal tonal gradient with coarse halftone grain and scattered dot texture like static. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: olive-brown and mustard yellow dominate, with one small deep rust-orange accent glinting off a chain link. Layered composition: distant dusk sky, mid-ground silhouetted overgrown trail, close-up grass-tangled sprocket in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
