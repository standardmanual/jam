# 02-04 진흙탕의 영광 — 6. 야생 동물의 털

풀숲을 헤치다 우연히 마찰을 일으킨 짐승의 흔적. 곰인지 고라니인지 알 수 없어 서늘해집니다.

**negative prompt**

smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**

A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a tuft of coarse wild animal fur snagged on a splintered fence post, the fur rendered as a pure flat dark-umber silhouette shape with no outline, its wispy strands fanned out and catching against the rough wood grain which is drawn in bold black ink linework. The snagged fur tuft sits at the exact center of the frame, a single strand pulled taut and trembling as if the animal only just brushed past. In the mid-ground behind it, the jagged black silhouette of dense wild grass and low scrub looms at the edge of a forest line, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline, with two small glinting mustard-yellow eye-like glints barely visible deep in the shadow of the treeline, deliberately unclear whether they belong to a bear or a deer. In the far background, a tense dusk sky rendered in a limited olive-brown to charcoal tonal gradient with coarse halftone grain and scattered dot texture like static. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: olive-brown and mustard yellow dominate, with one small deep rust-orange accent on the fence post's exposed splinter. Layered composition: distant tense dusk sky, mid-ground silhouetted treeline, close-up snagged fur in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
