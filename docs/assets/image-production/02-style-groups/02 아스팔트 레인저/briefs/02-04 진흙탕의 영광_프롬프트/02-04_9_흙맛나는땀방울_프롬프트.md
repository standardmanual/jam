# 02-04 진흙탕의 영광 — 9. 흙 맛 나는 땀방울

앞사람이 튀긴 흙탕물이 입으로 들어왔을 때, 뱉지 않고 쿨하게 삼켜버린 야생의 짭짤함입니다.

**negative prompt**

smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**

A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a single large droplet frozen mid-air just before striking a partial silhouette of a rider's lower face and open mouth, seen only as a jaw and chin silhouette with no identifiable features, the droplet rendered in flat mustard-yellow with a murky olive-brown swirl mixed through its core like sweat mingled with splashed mud. A few smaller matching droplets trail behind it in the air. The droplet sits at the exact center of the frame. In the mid-ground behind it, the jagged black silhouette of a rider pedaling just ahead on a muddy trail, rear wheel throwing up a fan of spray, looms as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a gritty midday trail sky rendered in a limited olive-brown to charcoal tonal gradient with coarse halftone grain and scattered dot texture like static. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the droplet and any solid-form elements; the rider and jaw silhouettes remain pure flat shapes with no outline. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: olive-brown and mustard yellow dominate, with one small deep rust-orange accent rimming the droplet's edge. Layered composition: distant gritty trail sky, mid-ground silhouetted rider throwing spray, close-up falling droplet in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
