# 02-05 야간 산악 행군 — 1. 방전된 헤드랜턴

칠흑 같은 산속에서 불이 꺼지는 순간의 공포를 머금은 껍데기. 여분 배터리를 안 챙긴 대가입니다.

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a battered head-lamp with its elastic strap coiled loosely around it, sitting dead center of the frame, its lens rendered as a flat dull amber-glass disc with the faint last flicker of light dying inside it as a thin cracking amber glow, the plastic housing in flat weathered indigo-grey with one dark shading tone along its edges. A couple of spent AA batteries lie discarded beside it, rendered as flat pale cylinder shapes. In the mid-ground behind the headlamp, the jagged black silhouette of a narrow mountain trail winding between looming pine trunks looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a moonless night sky roils with a limited indigo-blue-to-charcoal tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, with a faint jagged ridgeline cutting across it. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the headlamp housing and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: deep indigo blue and forest green dominate, with one small glowing amber accent on the dying lens flicker. Layered composition: distant indigo night sky and ridgeline, mid-ground silhouetted forest trail, close-up dying headlamp in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
