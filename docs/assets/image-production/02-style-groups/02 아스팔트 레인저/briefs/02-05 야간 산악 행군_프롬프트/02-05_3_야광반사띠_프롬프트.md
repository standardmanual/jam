# 02-05 야간 산악 행군 — 3. 야광 반사 띠

헤드라이트 불빛에 번쩍이며 서로의 생존을 확인시켜 주던 생명줄. 달빛 아래서 은은하게 빛납니다.

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a length of reflective safety tape wound loosely into a coiled loop, sitting at the exact center of the frame, the fabric band rendered in flat forest-green with one dark shading tone along its folds, the reflective strip running through it catching a cold glow rendered as a bright pale-amber gleaming line. The coil casts a faint scattering of small glinting flecks around it, rendered as tiny flat pale dots. In the mid-ground behind the coil, the jagged black silhouette of a steep forested mountain trail looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a moonlit night sky roils with a limited indigo-blue-to-charcoal tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, with a pale crescent moon glowing faintly. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the tape coil and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: deep indigo blue and forest green dominate, with one small glowing amber accent along the reflective gleam. Layered composition: distant indigo moonlit sky, mid-ground silhouetted forested trail, close-up coiled reflective tape in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
