# 02-05 야간 산악 행군 — 9. 별빛 한 조각

가로등 하나 없는 능선에 누워 바라본 쏟아지는 은하수. 도시에서는 절대 주울 수 없는 조각입니다.

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a dense sweeping band of the milky way arcing directly through the middle of the frame, countless stars rendered as tiny flat pale-cream dots of varying size clustered densely along the band, with a few larger stars rendered as small flat amber points glowing brighter than the rest. The star band curves just above a jagged mountain ridgeline. In the mid-ground, the jagged black silhouette of a bare mountain ridge with a few lone wind-bent pines looms along the lower edge, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. The surrounding night sky fills most of the frame, rendered with a limited indigo-blue-to-deep-charcoal tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, deepest and darkest toward the corners. Bold, consistent black ink outlines are absent on the stars and silhouette, kept as pure flat shapes. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, the deep indigo sky itself serving as the charcoal-black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: deep indigo blue and forest green dominate the sky and ridge, with several small glowing amber accents scattered as the brightest stars. Layered composition: sweeping star band and indigo night sky filling the frame, mid-ground silhouetted ridge and pines along the base — the star cluster centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
