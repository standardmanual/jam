# 02-06 망가진 기어들의 무덤 — 1. 끊어진 변속 와이어

업힐에서 댄싱을 치는 순간 '툭' 하는 소리와 함께 허벅지 근육을 파괴한 배신자입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 끊어진 자전거 변속 와이어)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a snapped bicycle shift cable, its steel strands frayed and splaying outward mid-air like a burst of wire fibers, still threaded through a battered derailleur housing rendered in flat gunmetal-gray with one dark shading tone. The frayed cable ends catch a rust-orange highlight where the metal has torn, the exposed inner core rendered as a bright saturated rust-orange accent against the dull gray housing. The cable sits at the exact center of the frame. In the mid-ground behind it, the flat black silhouette of a cyclist stands out of the saddle in a hard dancing climb on a steep switchback road, legs mid-stroke, rendered as pure flat silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a heavy overcast sky looms with a limited charcoal-to-warm-gray tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the cable and housing. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: weathered steel-gray and concrete-gray dominate, with rust-orange as the singular searing accent on the torn wire. Layered composition: distant stormy sky, mid-ground silhouetted climbing cyclist, close-up snapped cable in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
