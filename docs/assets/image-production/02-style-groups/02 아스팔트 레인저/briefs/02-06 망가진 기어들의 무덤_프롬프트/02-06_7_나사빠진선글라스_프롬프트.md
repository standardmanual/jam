# 02-06 망가진 기어들의 무덤 — 7. 나사 빠진 선글라스

덜컹거리는 다운힐에서 나사 하나를 흘려 렌즈가 달랑거리는 비운의 고글 파편입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 나사 빠진 스포츠 선글라스)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a pair of sport sunglasses with one lens hanging loose by a single hinge, the empty screw hole rendered as a small dark void rimmed with a thin bright rust-orange scuff, the frame rendered in flat weathered gray with one dark shading tone and the dangling lens in translucent pale-gray with a faint limited gradient. The glasses sit at the exact center of the frame, tilted as if caught mid-bounce. In the mid-ground behind it, the flat black silhouette of a rocky downhill mountain trail drops away steeply, loose boulders scattered along the path, rendered as pure flat silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a heavy dusk sky churns with a limited charcoal-to-warm-gray tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the frame and hinge. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: warm gray and steel-gray dominate, with one small rust-orange accent marking the stripped screw hole. Layered composition: distant dusk sky, mid-ground silhouetted rocky descent, close-up loose-lens sunglasses in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
