# 02-06 망가진 기어들의 무덤 — 8. 마모된 클릿

계단을 오르내리다 닳고 닳아 더 이상 페달과 결합되지 않는 플라스틱. 펭귄 걸음의 원인입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 마모된 자전거 클릿)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a worn-down cycling cleat bolted to the sole of a shoe, its once-sharp engagement teeth rounded and smoothed into a flat rust-orange-scuffed nub, the plastic body rendered in flat weathered gray with one dark shading tone showing the grooves worn away by countless steps. The cleat sits at the exact center of the frame, sole facing outward. In the mid-ground behind it, the flat black silhouette of a long concrete stairwell descends at an angle, railing and steps rendered as pure flat silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a flat gray afternoon sky stretches with a limited charcoal-to-ash-gray tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the cleat and sole edge. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: ash-gray and concrete-gray dominate, with rust-orange as the singular accent on the worn engagement points. Layered composition: distant flat sky, mid-ground silhouetted stairwell, close-up worn cleat in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
