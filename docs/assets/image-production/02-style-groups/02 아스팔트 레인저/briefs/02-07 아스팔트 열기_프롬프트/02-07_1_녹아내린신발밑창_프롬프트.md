# 02-07 아스팔트 열기 — 1. 녹아내린 신발 밑창

섭씨 40도로 끓어오르는 아스팔트 위를 달리다 끈적하게 눌어붙어 버린 고무의 눈물입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 녹아 흘러내린 러닝화 밑창)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a single running shoe sole peeled away from its shoe, its rubber tread drooping and stretching into thick melted strands that fuse into the cracked asphalt beneath it, rendered as bold flat amber-orange rubber with a few deep red molten streaks glowing at the point of contact. The sole sits at the exact center of the frame, flat warm-amber color with one dark shading tone along its ridges, the melted strands rendered as smooth flowing ink shapes. Faint heat-shimmer lines ripple upward around it as flat pale wavy bands. In the mid-ground, the jagged black silhouette of a sun-baked city street stretches back with cracked pavement lines and the low boxy silhouettes of parked cars, rendered as pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a relentless midday sky burns with a limited amber-to-deep-red tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like heat static. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the shoe sole and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: amber dominates, with one small deep red accent glowing where the rubber meets the asphalt. Layered composition: scorching amber sky, mid-ground silhouetted street, close-up melted sole in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
