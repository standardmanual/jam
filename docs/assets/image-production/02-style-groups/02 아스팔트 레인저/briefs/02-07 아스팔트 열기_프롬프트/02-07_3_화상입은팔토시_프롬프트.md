# 02-07 아스팔트 열기 — 3. 화상 입은 팔 토시

자외선을 막으려 꼈으나 오히려 찜질기가 되어버린, 땀에 전 축축한 쿨토시입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 땀에 젖고 열자국이 남은 팔 토시)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a single arm cooling sleeve pulled off and lying twisted on the ground, its fabric rendered as flat weathered amber with dark damp patches of sweat soaked through the weave, a few small deep red welt-like blotches marking scorched skin-heat marks along its length. The sleeve sits at the exact center of the frame, flat color with one dark shading tone tracing its wrinkles and folds. Faint heat-shimmer lines rise off the fabric as thin flat pale wavy bands. In the mid-ground behind it, the black silhouette of a partial figure's forearm and bent elbow reaches across the frame, shown only as a pure flat silhouette with no outline, no face or identifying detail, alongside the jagged silhouette of a sun-baked overpass railing. In the far background, a merciless afternoon sky glows with a limited amber-to-deep-red tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like heat static. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the sleeve and any solid-form elements; the arm silhouette stays outline-free. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: amber dominates, with one small deep red accent on the welt marks. Layered composition: burning amber sky, mid-ground silhouetted arm and railing, close-up damp sleeve in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
