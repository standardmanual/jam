# 02-07 아스팔트 열기 — 4. 증발해 버린 땀방울

맺히자마자 열기에 말라붙어 피부에 소금 결정을 남긴 짜디짠 한여름의 훈장입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 소금 결정과 함께 증발하는 땀방울)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a single large sweat droplet caught mid-evaporation, its lower half already crystallizing into a jagged patch of pale salt crystals while its upper half thins into a wisp of rising steam, rendered as flat pale amber-white droplet shape with sharp angular salt-crystal facets in flat cream tone and one deep red glint of light refracting through the droplet's surface. The droplet and its salt patch sit at the exact center of the frame, resting on a sliver of sun-scorched skin-like surface rendered as flat warm amber with one dark shading tone. Thin flat heat-shimmer lines curl upward around it. In the mid-ground, the black silhouette of a partial figure's shoulder and jawline tilts back, shown only as a pure flat silhouette with no outline, no identifiable face, against the jagged silhouette of distant rooftops. In the far background, a punishing high-noon sky burns with a limited amber-to-deep-red tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like heat static. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the droplet and crystal forms; the figure silhouette stays outline-free. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: amber and cream dominate, with one small deep red accent glinting in the droplet. Layered composition: scorching amber sky, mid-ground silhouetted figure and rooftops, close-up evaporating droplet in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
