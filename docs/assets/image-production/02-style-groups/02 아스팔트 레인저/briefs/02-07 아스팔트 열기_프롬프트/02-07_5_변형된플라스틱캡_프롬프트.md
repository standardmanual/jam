# 02-07 아스팔트 열기 — 5. 변형된 플라스틱 캡

뜨거운 햇살 아래 방치되었다가 찌그러져 다시는 원래 모양으로 돌아오지 않는 불쌍한 뚜껑입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 열에 뒤틀려 찌그러진 플라스틱 병뚜껑)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a single plastic bottle cap warped out of its original round shape into a lopsided, sagging oval, one edge drooping and pinched shut where the heat melted it, rendered as flat burnt-amber plastic with one dark shading tone tracing the sagging fold and a small deep red scorch mark blistered into its surface. The cap sits at the exact center of the frame atop a sliver of cracked sun-baked asphalt rendered as flat charcoal-amber with fine crack linework. Thin flat heat-shimmer lines ripple upward around it. In the mid-ground, the jagged black silhouette of a roadside guardrail and a row of wilting curbside weeds looms, rendered as pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a merciless midday sky burns with a limited amber-to-deep-red tonal gradient radiating from a small flat sun disc, coarse halftone grain and tiny scattered dot texture like heat static throughout. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the cap and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: amber dominates, with one small deep red accent on the scorch mark. Layered composition: blazing amber sky, mid-ground silhouetted guardrail and weeds, close-up warped cap in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
