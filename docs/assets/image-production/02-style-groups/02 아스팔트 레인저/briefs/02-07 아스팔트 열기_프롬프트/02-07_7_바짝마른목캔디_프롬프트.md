# 02-07 아스팔트 열기 — 7. 바짝 마른 목캔디

더위를 식히기 위해 입에 넣었으나 침마저 말라버려 목구멍을 턱 막히게 한 건조한 사탕입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 바싹 말라 갈라진 목캔디)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a single round throat lozenge sitting on a sun-cracked surface, its once-smooth face now dry and fissured with fine crack lines radiating outward like a drought-parched riverbed, rendered as flat pale amber candy with one dark shading tone tracing the cracks and a small deep red foil-wrapper fragment curling away beside it. The lozenge sits at the exact center of the frame, a thin flat wisp of hot dry air curling upward around it as pale wavy lines. In the mid-ground behind it, the black silhouette of a partial figure's tilted-back throat and jaw looms, shown only as a pure flat silhouette with no outline, no identifiable face, beside the jagged silhouette of a heat-cracked road stretching away. In the far background, a punishing dry-season sky burns with a limited amber-to-deep-red tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like heat static throughout. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the lozenge and wrapper fragment; the figure silhouette stays outline-free. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: amber dominates, with one small deep red accent on the wrapper fragment. Layered composition: scorching amber sky, mid-ground silhouetted figure and road, close-up cracked lozenge in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
