# 02-08 상처뿐인 영광 — 1. 피 묻은 반창고

자전거에서 넘어지거나 돌부리에 채인 후, 급하게 편의점에서 사 붙인 눈물겨운 응급처치입니다.

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a single adhesive bandage strip applied hastily across a scraped knuckle, a small blot of dried blood soaking through the pad at its center, rendered as a saturated crimson stain with jagged, organic edges against the pale off-white gauze pad. The bandage sits at the exact center of the frame, its fabric strip rendered in flat steel-gray with one dark shading tone along the folded edges, the adhesive backing peeling slightly at one corner. Beside it, a crumpled convenience-store bandage wrapper lies torn open, rendered as flat white paper with sharp black ink creases. In the mid-ground behind the bandage, the jagged black silhouette of a cracked asphalt curb and a fallen bicycle wheel looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a heavy overcast sky roils with a limited charcoal-to-steel-gray tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the bandage, wrapper, and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: steel gray and white dominate, with one small intense crimson accent at the blood stain. Layered composition: distant overcast sky, mid-ground silhouetted curb and wheel, close-up bandage in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
