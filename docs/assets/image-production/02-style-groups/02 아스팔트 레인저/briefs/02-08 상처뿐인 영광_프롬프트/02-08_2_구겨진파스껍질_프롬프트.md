# 02-08 상처뿐인 영광 — 2. 구겨진 파스 껍질

허벅지와 종아리에서 불타오르는 근육통을 잠재우기 위해 뜯어낸 파스의 서늘한 잔해입니다.

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a used menthol pain-relief patch peeled away from skin, its rectangular pad rendered in flat white with a faint grid of tiny cooling vents, resting atop a crumpled sheet of glossy patch backing film rendered in flat steel-gray with sharp angular creases catching the light. Faint wisps of cooling vapor curl upward from the patch's surface, rendered as thin pale steel-gray streaks. A single smear of deep crimson liniment gel glistens along one edge of the patch where it was hastily torn off. The patch and crumpled backing sit at the exact center of the frame. In the mid-ground behind them, the jagged black silhouette of a runner's calf and thigh muscles, tensed mid-stride, looms as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a flat evening sky roils with a limited charcoal-to-steel-gray tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the patch, backing film, and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: steel gray and white dominate this coolest, palest piece of the set, with one small intense crimson accent on the liniment smear. Layered composition: distant dusk sky, mid-ground silhouetted running legs, close-up crumpled patch in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
