# 02-08 상처뿐인 영광 — 7. 부러진 손톱

넘어지며 땅을 짚다 그만 날아가 버린 손톱. 키보드를 칠 때마다 그날의 아픔이 떠오릅니다.

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a single fingertip pressed flat against cracked asphalt, shown only as a pure black silhouette with no other hand or arm detail visible, one fingernail torn cleanly away and rendered separately beside it as a small pale white curved fragment with a sharp black ink outline, a thin crescent of crimson-red exposed at the nail bed where it tore free. Fine cracks radiate outward from the fingertip across the pavement, rendered as sharp bright linework. The fingertip and broken nail sit at the exact center of the frame. In the mid-ground behind them, the jagged black silhouette of a fallen bicycle frame and scattered gravel looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a flat overcast sky roils with a limited charcoal-to-steel-gray tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the broken nail fragment and any solid-form elements, with the fingertip itself left as a pure outline-free silhouette. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: steel gray and white dominate, with one small intense crimson accent at the exposed nail bed. Layered composition: distant overcast sky, mid-ground silhouetted bicycle and gravel, close-up broken nail in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic gore, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
