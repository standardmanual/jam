# 02-09 생존 배낭의 밑바닥 — 3. 다 쓴 정수 알약

계곡물을 떠 마셔야 했던 절박한 순간, 배탈의 공포를 이겨내기 위해 투하한 화학약품 빈 통입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 텅 빈 정수 알약 통)

**negative prompt**

smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**

A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a small tipped-over water purification tablet tube, its cap fallen loose beside it and a few last flat round tablets spilling out, rendered in a flat khaki-brown plastic tone with one darker umber shading tone along the tube's curve. The tube sits at the exact center of the frame. In the mid-ground behind it, the black silhouette of cupped hands scoops water from a rocky creek, ripples radiating outward as thin flat pale lines, the creek stones and rushing water rendered as pure flat black silhouette shapes with no outline. In the far background, a dense forest canopy of overlapping tree silhouettes frames a narrow gorge, above which a limited khaki-to-mustard tonal gradient sky glows with coarse halftone grain and scattered dot texture like fine mist. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the tube and tablets. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: khaki and warm brown dominate, with one small mustard-yellow accent on the tube's faded label patch. Layered composition: distant forest canopy and gorge, mid-ground silhouetted cupped hands at the creek, close-up tipped tablet tube in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
