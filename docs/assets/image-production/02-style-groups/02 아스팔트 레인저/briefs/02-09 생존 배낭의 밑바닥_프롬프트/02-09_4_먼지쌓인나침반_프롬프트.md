# 02-09 생존 배낭의 밑바닥 — 4. 먼지 쌓인 나침반

스마트폰 배터리가 꺼진 후, 떨리는 손으로 꺼내 북쪽을 찾던 아날로그 생존의 길잡이입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 낡고 먼지 쌓인 아날로그 나침반)

**negative prompt**

smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**

A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on an old analog compass, its brass casing scuffed and dulled, a fine layer of dust rendered as scattered pale speckle texture across the glass, the needle frozen mid-quiver pointing north drawn as a bold sharp red-tipped line. The compass sits at the exact center of the frame, its casing in flat weathered khaki-brass tone with one dark umber shading tone along the rim. A silhouetted trembling hand holds the compass up from below, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline, fingers slightly splayed. In the mid-ground behind it, the jagged black silhouette of an endless mountain ridge line stretches toward the horizon, empty and directionless. In the far background, a vast overcast sky rolls with a limited khaki-to-mustard-brown tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the compass casing and needle. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: khaki-brass and brown dominate, with one small deep-red accent on the trembling needle tip. Layered composition: distant overcast sky, mid-ground silhouetted ridge line, close-up dusty compass in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
