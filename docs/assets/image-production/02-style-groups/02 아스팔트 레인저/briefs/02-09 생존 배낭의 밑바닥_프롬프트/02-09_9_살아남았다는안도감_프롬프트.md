# 02-09 생존 배낭의 밑바닥 — 9. 살아남았다는 안도감

무사히 집 현관문을 열고 들어왔을 때, 무거운 배낭을 내던지며 내뱉은 깊고 진한 한숨입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 문 앞에 주저앉아 배낭을 내려놓는 지친 실루엣)

**negative prompt**

smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**

A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a heavy backpack dropped and slumped onto a wooden porch step, its straps splayed loosely, rendered in flat weathered khaki-brown tones with one darker umber shading tone along its collapsed folds. Beside it at the exact center of the frame, the pure black silhouette of an exhausted hiker sits slumped against a cabin door frame, head tipped back, shoulders sagging in a long exhale, rendered as a flat silhouette shape with no outline. The wooden door and porch beams are rendered with bold, consistent black ink outlines and flat mustard-brown planks. In the mid-ground behind the porch, the black silhouette of a small mountain cabin roofline and a lone leafless tree stand against the coming light. In the far background, a hazy dawn sky glows with a limited khaki-to-mustard tonal gradient, coarse halftone grain and scattered dot texture like morning mist. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: khaki and warm brown dominate, with one small mustard-yellow accent glowing along the dawn horizon line. Layered composition: distant dawn sky and cabin silhouette, mid-ground rooftree, close-up slumped figure and dropped backpack in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
