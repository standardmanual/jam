# 02-10 태풍의 눈 — 4. 바람에 날아온 신문지

시야가 가려진 찰나, 축축한 신문지가 헬멧에 달라붙어 시야를 100% 차단한 공포의 순간입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 헬멧 바이저에 달라붙은 젖은 신문지)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a cycling helmet visor completely plastered flat with a soaked sheet of newspaper, the wet paper rendered in flat pale gray with faint blotchy dark-gray patches suggesting smeared, illegible print (no actual letters), water droplets beading and dripping off its curled edges as flat pale shapes. The helmet visor beneath is rendered in flat weathered teal with one dark shading tone along its curve, sitting at the exact center of the frame. A few more loose sheets of wet paper flap in the air around it. In the mid-ground behind it, the jagged black silhouette of a rain-lashed street with a toppled newsstand looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a heavy typhoon sky roils with a limited charcoal-to-teal tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, with faint streaks of driving rain. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the helmet and any solid-form elements; the paper itself stays outline-free with only tonal creases. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: pale gray and dusty teal dominate, with one small acid-yellow accent on the helmet's reflective strip peeking from under the paper. Layered composition: distant stormy street sky, mid-ground silhouetted newsstand, close-up paper-plastered visor in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
