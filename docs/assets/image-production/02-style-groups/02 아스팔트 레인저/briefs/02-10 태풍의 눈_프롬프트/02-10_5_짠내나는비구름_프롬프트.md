# 02-10 태풍의 눈 — 5. 짠 내 나는 비구름

해안가에서 몰아친 태풍이 바닷물을 머금고 내륙으로 쏟아낸, 입술에 닿은 짭짤한 빗물의 파편입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 해안에서 몰아쳐 오는 비구름과 튀는 바닷물 빗방울)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a dense, low-hanging rain cloud rolling in from the sea, its underside rendered as a flat heavy teal-gray mass with one darker shading tone, trailing a curtain of fat raindrops and fine wind-blown sea spray depicted as small flat pale droplet shapes converging toward the center of the frame. A scatter of tiny salt-crystal specks glints faintly among the droplets as small pale dots. The cloud and its rain curtain sit at the exact center of the composition. In the mid-ground behind it, the jagged black silhouette of a wild coastal shoreline with crashing whitecap waves looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a vast typhoon sky roils with a limited charcoal-to-teal tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the cloud mass and any solid-form elements; the spray droplets remain outline-free flat shapes. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: deep teal and slate gray dominate, with one small acid-yellow accent glinting on a distant coastal warning buoy light. Layered composition: distant vast storm sky, mid-ground silhouetted crashing coastline, close-up rolling rain cloud with salt spray in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
