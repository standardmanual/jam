# 02-02 폭우 속의 질주 — 2. 흙탕물 튄 고글

앞차의 뒷바퀴에서 뿜어져 나오는 흙탕물을 정통으로 맞은 흔적. 시야 포기 선언과도 같습니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 흙탕물이 튄 고글)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a pair of riding goggles, its lens sprayed almost opaque with muddy water flung up from a road, the mud rendered as flat murky slate-gray splatter blobs radiating across the glass in a burst pattern. The goggle frame is flat off-white with one dark shading tone, and a few thick droplets of dirty water drip from its lower rim, frozen mid-fall as flat polygon shapes. One small crack of clear lens cutting through the mud catches a sharp deep red highlight, the only warm color in the frame. The goggles sit at the exact center of the frame, well clear of the four corners. In the mid-ground behind them, the black silhouette of a motorcycle's rear wheel kicking up a fan of spray along a guardrail-lined road looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a heavy storm sky churns with a limited slate-gray-to-charcoal tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, streaked with driving rain lines. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the goggles and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: slate gray and off-white dominate, with one small deep red accent on the cracked lens. Layered composition: distant stormy sky, mid-ground silhouetted spraying wheel, close-up mud-splattered goggles in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
