# 02-02 폭우 속의 질주 — 4. 침수된 후미등

빗물이 스며들어 깜빡거림을 멈춘 붉은 불빛. 오늘 밤은 당신의 생존 본능만이 유일한 안전장치입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 빗물이 차오른 후미등)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a motorcycle taillight housing, its clear lens fogged and half-filled with seeped rainwater, the trapped water rendered as a flat pale slate-gray pool with a faint tide-line shading, inside of which a single bulb glows a dim, dying deep red — the one warm color in the frame, its light diffused and weak rather than bright. The housing's plastic casing is flat weathered off-white with one dark shading tone, streaked with rivulets of rain rendered as thin flat white lines. A few heavy raindrops cling to and drip from the lens edge, frozen as flat polygon droplets. The taillight sits at the exact center of the frame, well clear of the four corners. In the mid-ground behind it, the black silhouette of a line of stalled vehicles queued before a flooded underpass looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a heavy storm sky churns with a limited slate-gray-to-charcoal tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, streaked with driving rain lines. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the taillight housing and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: slate gray and off-white dominate, with the dying deep red glow as the sole accent. Layered composition: distant stormy sky, mid-ground silhouetted flooded underpass, close-up flooded taillight in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
