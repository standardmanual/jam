# 02-02 폭우 속의 질주 — 6. 불어터진 손가락 밴드

빗물에 퉁퉁 불어 상처에서 떨어져 나간 밴드. 고통 따위는 빗물에 씻어 보낸 지 오래입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 손가락에서 벗겨지는 불어터진 밴드)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a close-up of a gloved hand rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline and no facial or personal detail, one bare finger extended from a torn glove tip, a swollen, water-logged adhesive bandage peeling loose from it. The bandage itself is flat off-white gone wrinkled and puffy from rain, curling away at one corner to reveal a small flat deep red mark beneath — the only warm color in the frame. Heavy raindrops streak down the finger and bandage in thin flat white lines, with a few droplets frozen mid-fall as flat polygon shapes. The hand and bandage sit at the exact center of the frame, well clear of the four corners. In the mid-ground behind it, the black silhouette of an overflowing roadside drainage ditch running alongside a guardrail looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a heavy storm sky churns with a limited slate-gray-to-charcoal tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, streaked with driving rain lines. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the bandage and any solid-form elements, while the gloved hand itself remains a clean outline-free silhouette. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: slate gray and off-white dominate, with one small deep red accent beneath the peeling bandage. Layered composition: distant stormy sky, mid-ground silhouetted ditch, close-up peeling bandage in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
