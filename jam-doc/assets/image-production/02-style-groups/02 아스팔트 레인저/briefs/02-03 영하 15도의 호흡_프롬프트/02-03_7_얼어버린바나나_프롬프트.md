# 02-03 영하 15도의 호흡 — 7. 얼어버린 바나나

에너지 보충을 위해 꺼냈으나 호신용 무기로 써도 될 만큼 단단해진 둔기형 과일입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 얼어붙어 몽둥이처럼 단단해진 바나나)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a single banana frozen rock-solid at the exact middle of the frame, its peel rendered in flat pale white with one dark ice-blue shading tone, a thin crust of frost and sharp icy crystals coating its curved surface like it has been forged into a club, a few hairline frost cracks glinting along its length. A short deep red twist-tie is wrapped tight around its stem end, the only saturated warm accent in the frame. A scatter of small frost crystals hover frozen mid-air around the banana, rendered as flat pale polygon shapes. In the mid-ground behind the banana, the jagged black silhouette of a frost-laced backpack strap and carabiner looms half in shadow, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a heavy overcast winter sky holds a limited charcoal-to-ice-blue tonal gradient with coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, and faint streaks of drifting snow. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the banana and twist-tie. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background reaching into all four corners. Vivid color contrast: ice blue and white dominate, with one small deep red accent on the twist-tie. Layered composition: distant overcast sky, mid-ground silhouetted backpack strap, close-up frozen banana in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
