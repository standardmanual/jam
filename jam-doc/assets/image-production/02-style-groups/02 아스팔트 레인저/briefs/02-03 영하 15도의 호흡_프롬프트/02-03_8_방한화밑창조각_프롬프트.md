# 02-03 영하 15도의 호흡 — 8. 방한화 밑창 조각

빙판길을 밟다 뜯어져 나간 고무 조각. 미끄러짐을 방지하려다 본인이 미끄러진 비운의 파편입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 얼음판 위에 떨어진 등산화 밑창 조각)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a torn fragment of a winter boot sole at the exact middle of the frame, its deep aggressive tread lugs rendered in flat white rubber with one dark ice-blue shading tone, the torn edge jagged and rough where it ripped free. A row of deep red rubber lugs is inlaid along the tread pattern, the only saturated warm accent in the frame. The fragment rests atop a sheet of cracked ice, with sharp bright crack lines radiating outward from where it skidded and fell, rendered as thin white linework across the flat ice-blue surface. In the mid-ground behind the fragment, the jagged black silhouette of a steep icy slope and a lone frost-covered guardrail looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a bitter cold sky holds a limited steel-blue to white tonal gradient with coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, and faint streaks of blowing snow. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the sole fragment and the ice-crack linework. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background reaching into all four corners. Vivid color contrast: ice blue and white dominate, with one small deep red accent on the tread lugs. Layered composition: distant cold sky, mid-ground silhouetted icy slope, close-up torn sole fragment in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
