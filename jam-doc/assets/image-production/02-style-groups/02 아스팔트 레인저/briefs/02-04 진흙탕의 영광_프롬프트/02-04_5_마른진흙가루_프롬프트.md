# 02-04 진흙탕의 영광 — 5. 마른 진흙 가루

며칠 전의 영광을 잊지 못하고 현관에 우수수 떨어져 등짝 스매싱을 유발하는 흙먼지입니다.

**negative prompt**

smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**

A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a cracked, sun-dried clump of mud crumbling apart mid-air, its surface a web of fine crack lines rendered in bold black ink over flat olive-brown, with a cloud of loose dry mud flakes and fine dust particles scattering outward from it in flat mustard-yellow speckled shapes, some flakes rendered as tiny angular chips catching the light. The crumbling mud clump hovers at the exact center of the frame, as if it just flaked off a boot sole onto a doorway floor below. In the mid-ground behind it, the jagged black silhouette of a doorway threshold and a pair of propped-up muddy boots looms faintly, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a dim indoor-entryway light glow rendered in a limited mustard-yellow to charcoal tonal gradient with coarse halftone grain and scattered dot texture like drifting dust motes. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the mud clump and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: olive-brown and mustard yellow dominate, with one small deep rust-orange accent on the largest dry mud chip. Layered composition: distant glowing entryway, mid-ground silhouetted doorway and boots, close-up crumbling dry mud in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
