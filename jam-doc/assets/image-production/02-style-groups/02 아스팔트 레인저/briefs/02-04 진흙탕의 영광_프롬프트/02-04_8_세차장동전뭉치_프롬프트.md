**생성 prompt**

A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a small loose stack of coins spilling out of a cupped hand shown only as a partial silhouette with no visible face or identity, the coins rendered in flat mustard-yellow metal with bold black ink outlines tracing their ridged edges, a couple of coins caught mid-fall and mid-spin below the stack. The coin pile sits at the exact center of the frame. In the mid-ground behind it, the jagged black silhouette of a coin-operated pressure-washer car wash bay looms, its hose nozzle and stall frame rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a hazy wash-bay sky rendered in a limited olive-brown to charcoal tonal gradient with coarse halftone grain and scattered dot texture like drifting spray mist. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the coins and any solid-form elements; the hand and wash-bay silhouettes remain pure flat shapes with no outline. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: olive-brown and mustard yellow dominate, with one small deep rust-orange accent glinting off the topmost coin's edge. Layered composition: distant hazy wash-bay sky, mid-ground silhouetted car wash stall, close-up spilling coins in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
