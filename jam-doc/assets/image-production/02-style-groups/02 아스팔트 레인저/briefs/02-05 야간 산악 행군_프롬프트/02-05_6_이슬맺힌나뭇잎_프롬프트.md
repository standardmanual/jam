# 02-05 야간 산악 행군 — 6. 이슬 맺힌 나뭇잎

새벽안개를 머금은 서늘한 잎사귀. 바스락거리는 발소리를 죽여주는 산속의 훌륭한 카페트입니다.

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a single broad fallen leaf heavy with dew, sitting at the exact center of the frame, the leaf rendered in flat forest-green with one dark shading tone along its veins, a cluster of small dewdrops beaded along its edge rendered as tiny flat pale-cream circles catching a cold gleam. A scatter of smaller dry leaves lies around it on the forest floor, rendered as flat dark-indigo silhouette shapes. In the mid-ground behind the leaf, the jagged black silhouette of a dense mountain forest floor lined with looming tree trunks looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a pre-dawn misty sky roils with a limited indigo-blue-to-charcoal tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, with faint drifting mist streaks. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the leaf and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: deep indigo blue and forest green dominate, with one small glowing amber accent on the largest dewdrop's gleam. Layered composition: distant indigo misty sky, mid-ground silhouetted forest floor, close-up dew-heavy leaf in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
