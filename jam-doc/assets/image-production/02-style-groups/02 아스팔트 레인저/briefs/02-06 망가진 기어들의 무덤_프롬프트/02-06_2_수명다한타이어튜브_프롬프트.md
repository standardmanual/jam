# 02-06 망가진 기어들의 무덤 — 2. 수명 다한 타이어 튜브

펑크 패치만 5개가 붙어 있어 더 이상 공기를 품을 수 없는 걸레짝이 된 고무 링입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 패치 붙은 자전거 타이어 튜브)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a limp, deflated bicycle inner tube coiled into a loose ring, its rubber surface dulled matte charcoal-gray and covered in five mismatched round patches rendered as flat rust-orange discs of varying size, each patch outlined with a thin darker ring to show layered repairs. The tube sags and folds on itself at the exact center of the frame, rendered in flat gray with one darker shading tone along the folds, no glossy highlights. In the mid-ground behind it, the flat black silhouette of a roadside bike wheel rim leans against a curb, tire hanging loose, rendered as pure flat silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a dim dusk sky stretches with a limited charcoal-to-ash-gray tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the tube and wheel rim. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: muted grays dominate, with rust-orange patches as the repeated searing accent scattered across the tube. Layered composition: distant dusk sky, mid-ground silhouetted wheel rim, close-up patched tube in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
