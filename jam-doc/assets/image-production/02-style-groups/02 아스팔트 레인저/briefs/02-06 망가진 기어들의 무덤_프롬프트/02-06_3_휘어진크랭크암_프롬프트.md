# 02-06 망가진 기어들의 무덤 — 3. 휘어진 크랭크 암

얼마나 무식한 파워로 밟아댔는지 쇳덩이가 엿가락처럼 휘어버린, 광기의 증명서입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 휘어진 자전거 크랭크 암)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a bent bicycle crank arm, the once-straight steel bar warped into a taffy-like curve, rendered in flat weathered gunmetal-gray with one dark shading tone along the bend and a bright rust-orange scuff mark where the metal buckled under force. The pedal still clings to its end, rendered as a flat gray block. The crank sits at the exact center of the frame, its curve dominating the composition. In the mid-ground behind it, the flat black silhouette of a cyclist sprints up a steep incline out of the saddle, body straining forward, rendered as pure flat silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a brooding sky rolls with a limited charcoal-to-slate-gray tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the crank and pedal. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: slate-gray and steel-gray dominate, with one bright rust-orange accent marking the point of failure on the bent metal. Layered composition: distant brooding sky, mid-ground silhouetted sprinting cyclist, close-up warped crank arm in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
