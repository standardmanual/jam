# 02-06 망가진 기어들의 무덤 — 4. 터진 에어 쿠션

푹신함을 제공하던 운동화의 에어가 터지며 '픽' 소리를 내고 사망한, 발바닥의 비극입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 터진 러닝화 에어쿠션)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a cutaway cross-section of a running shoe sole, its clear air cushion pocket ruptured and collapsed, the membrane torn open with jagged flat rust-orange shapes bursting outward like a small explosion frozen mid-motion, the surrounding sole foam rendered in flat weathered gray with one dark shading tone. The ruptured cushion sits at the exact center of the frame. In the mid-ground behind it, the flat black silhouette of a lone runner's legs stride across a long empty trail stretching toward the horizon, rendered as pure flat silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a vast dawn sky spreads with a limited charcoal-to-ash-gray tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the sole and cushion. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: ash-gray and concrete-gray dominate, with a burst of rust-orange as the singular accent at the point of rupture. Layered composition: distant dawn sky, mid-ground silhouetted runner, close-up burst air cushion in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
