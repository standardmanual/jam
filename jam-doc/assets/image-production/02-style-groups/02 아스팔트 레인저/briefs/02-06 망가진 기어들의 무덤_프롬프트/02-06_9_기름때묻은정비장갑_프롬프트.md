# 02-06 망가진 기어들의 무덤 — 9. 기름때 묻은 정비 장갑

길거리에서 체인이 빠졌을 때, 손을 더럽히지 않기 위해 장렬히 희생된 시커먼 면장갑입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 기름때 묻은 정비용 면장갑)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a single cotton work glove lying flat and splayed, its fingertips and palm blackened with thick grease stains rendered as flat charcoal-gray smears over a base of weathered gray fabric, one dark shading tone along the folds, and a faint rust-orange smudge where rust-colored chain grime has mixed into the grease. The glove sits at the exact center of the frame, fingers curled slightly as if just dropped. In the mid-ground behind it, the flat black silhouette of a bicycle chain hangs loose off a derailleur beside a curb, rendered as pure flat silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a hazy street-lit dusk sky glows with a limited charcoal-to-warm-gray tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the glove and chain. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: warm gray and charcoal-gray dominate, with a small rust-orange smudge as the singular accent bleeding through the grease stains. Layered composition: distant hazy street sky, mid-ground silhouetted loose chain, close-up grease-stained glove in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
