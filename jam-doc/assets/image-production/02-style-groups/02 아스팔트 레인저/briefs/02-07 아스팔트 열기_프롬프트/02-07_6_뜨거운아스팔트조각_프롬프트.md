# 02-07 아스팔트 열기 — 6. 뜨거운 아스팔트 조각

운동화 밑창에 박혀 집 안까지 따라 들어온, 열기를 가득 머금은 까만 돌멩이입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 신발 트레드 홈에 박힌 뜨거운 아스팔트 조각)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a jagged chunk of black asphalt lodged deep in the tread grooves of a shoe sole, rendered as flat charcoal-black stone with sharp angular facets and one deep red glowing crack running through its core like retained heat, the surrounding rubber tread in flat weathered amber with one dark shading tone. The lodged fragment sits at the exact center of the frame, thin flat heat-shimmer lines rippling off it as pale wavy bands. In the mid-ground behind it, the black silhouette of a cracked sidewalk stretching toward a low doorway looms, rendered as pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a scorching late-afternoon sky burns with a limited amber-to-deep-red tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like heat static throughout. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the stone fragment, tread, and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: amber and charcoal-black dominate, with one small deep red accent glowing in the stone's crack. Layered composition: burning amber sky, mid-ground silhouetted sidewalk and doorway, close-up lodged asphalt fragment in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
