# 02-07 아스팔트 열기 — 8. 아지랑이의 환영

오르막길 끝에 오아시스가 보인다고 믿게 만든, 뇌가 열에 익어버린 순간의 환각입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 오르막길 끝 아지랑이 속에 어른거리는 오아시스 신기루)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a wavering column of heat-shimmer rising from the crest of an uphill road, within which a ghostly, half-transparent mirage flickers into view — a cluster of drooping palm-like fronds and a pool of still water, rendered as faint flat pale-amber outlined shapes layered thinly over the shimmer, deliberately unstable and dreamlike compared to the solid forms around it, with one small deep red glow pulsing at its heart like a heatstroke afterimage. The mirage sits at the exact center of the frame atop the cracked asphalt crest. In the mid-ground, the black silhouette of a partial figure trudging uphill leans forward, shown only as a pure flat silhouette with no outline, no identifiable face, beside the jagged silhouette of the steep road shoulder and roadside signage posts (blank, no text). In the far background, a merciless high-noon sky burns with a limited amber-to-deep-red tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like heat static throughout. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the road and solid-form elements; the figure silhouette and mirage stay outline-free and translucent. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: amber dominates, with one small deep red accent glowing within the mirage. Layered composition: blazing amber sky, mid-ground silhouetted figure and road, close-up shimmering mirage in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
