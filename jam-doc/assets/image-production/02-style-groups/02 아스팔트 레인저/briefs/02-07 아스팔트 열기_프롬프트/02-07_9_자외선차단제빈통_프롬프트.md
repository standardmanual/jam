# 02-07 아스팔트 열기 — 9. 자외선 차단제 빈 통

미친 듯이 덧발라 하얀 가부키 화장이 되었지만 결국 새카맣게 타버린 슬픈 현실의 증거입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 하얀 크림 자국과 함께 짜부라진 빈 선크림 통)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a squeezed-flat sunscreen tube lying on its side with its cap popped open, a thick smear of pale cream oozing out and trailing across the ground where its edges have already dried and darkened into a scorched deep red-brown, rendered as flat cream-white cream against the tube's flat burnt-amber plastic body with one dark shading tone along its collapsed folds. The tube sits at the exact center of the frame. In the mid-ground behind it, the black silhouette of a partial figure's sunburned shoulder and streaked forearm looms, shown only as a pure flat silhouette with no outline, no identifiable face, beside the jagged silhouette of a beach-town rooftop line. In the far background, a merciless high-noon sky burns with a limited amber-to-deep-red tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like heat static throughout. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the tube and any solid-form elements; the figure silhouette stays outline-free. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: amber and cream dominate, with one small deep red accent where the dried cream has scorched dark. Layered composition: blazing amber sky, mid-ground silhouetted figure and rooftops, close-up collapsed tube in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
