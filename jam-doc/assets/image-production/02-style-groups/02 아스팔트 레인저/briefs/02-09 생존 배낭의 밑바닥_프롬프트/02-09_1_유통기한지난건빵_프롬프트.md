# 02-09 생존 배낭의 밑바닥 — 1. 유통기한 지난 건빵

배낭 구석에 박아두고 까맣게 잊고 있다가, 허기에 지쳐 꺼내 씹은 퍼석한 밀가루 블럭입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 딱딱하게 굳은 건빵 블록)

**negative prompt**

smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**

A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a cracked, bone-dry hardtack biscuit block sitting at the exact center of the frame, its surface rendered with hairline fracture lines and a few crumbled fragments and dust scattered around it, the biscuit in a flat dusty khaki-tan color with one darker brown shading tone along its broken edge. A silhouetted hand reaches into frame from below, fingers just closing around the biscuit, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the mid-ground, the slumped black silhouette of an exhausted hiker sits against a jagged rock outcrop, head bowed, knees drawn up. In the far background, a vast dusk mountain ridge rolls beneath a limited khaki-to-mustard-brown tonal gradient sky with coarse halftone grain and scattered dust-like static texture. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the biscuit and rock forms. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: khaki and warm brown dominate, with one small mustard-yellow accent glowing faintly at the biscuit's broken crumb edge. Layered composition: distant dusk ridge, mid-ground silhouetted figure, close-up cracked biscuit in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
