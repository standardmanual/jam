# 02-09 생존 배낭의 밑바닥 — 5. 마지막 성냥개비

젖은 손으로 긋다 수차례 실패하고 간신히 불씨를 피워낸, 기적의 빨간 머리 막대기입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 막 불이 붙은 성냥개비 하나)

**negative prompt**

smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**

A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a single lit matchstick, its thin wooden body in flat khaki-tan color, a small flame flaring at its tip rendered in bold saturated deep-red and mustard-yellow flat shapes with sharp jagged edges, a few spent charred matchsticks scattered loosely around its base as flat dark-brown silhouette slivers. The lit match sits at the exact center of the frame, held between two silhouetted fingers rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the mid-ground behind it, the black silhouette of a crouched hooded figure leans in close, hands cupped protectively around the flame, shoulders hunched against unseen wind. In the far background, a dense black forest silhouette presses in beneath a limited charcoal-to-khaki-brown tonal gradient night sky with coarse halftone grain and scattered dot texture like drifting ash. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the matchstick and spent matches. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: khaki-brown dominates, with the small flame itself as the vivid mustard-yellow and deep-red focal accent. Layered composition: distant dark forest and night sky, mid-ground hooded silhouette, close-up lit match in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
