# 02-10 태풍의 눈 — 3. 강풍에 찢긴 깃발

교량 위에 걸려있던 깃발이 폭풍우를 견디지 못하고 찢겨 날아와 얼굴을 강타한 조각입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 강풍에 찢겨 날아오는 깃발 조각)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a jagged, wind-torn fragment of a flag whipping through the air, its frayed edge rendered as sharp bright tears against a flat plain of muted teal fabric with one dark fold-shading tone, a snapped rope fragment still knotted to one corner. The flag fragment sits at the exact center of the frame, caught mid-tumble. A few loose threads and small debris scraps trail behind it as flat pale shapes. In the mid-ground behind it, the jagged black silhouette of a suspension bridge cable tower in the middle of a storm looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a heavy typhoon sky roils with a limited charcoal-to-teal tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static, with faint diagonal rain streaks. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the flag fragment and any solid-form elements. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: muted teal and slate gray dominate, with one small acid-yellow accent as a torn warning-stripe edge on the fabric. Layered composition: distant stormy bridge sky, mid-ground silhouetted bridge tower, close-up torn flag fragment in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
