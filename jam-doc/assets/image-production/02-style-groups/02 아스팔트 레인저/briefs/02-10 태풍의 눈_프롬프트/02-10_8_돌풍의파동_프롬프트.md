# 02-10 태풍의 눈 — 8. 돌풍의 파동

자전거째로 옆으로 밀려나게 만드는 무자비한 측풍. 뼈저리게 느낀 자연의 거대한 힘입니다. (생성해야 하는 표현: 옆으로 미끄러지는 자전거 바퀴 자국과 눈에 보이지 않는 돌풍을 형상화한 파동선)

**negative prompt**
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, photographic realism, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, circular badge border, ring frame outline, text, lettering, words, letters, typography, logo, watermark, signature, purple, violet, lavender hues

**생성 prompt**
A dark, dramatic lithograph-style illustration centered on a bicycle's rear wheel caught mid-skid, wrenched sideways by an unseen crosswind, its tire leaving a bold sharply curved black skid-mark arc across the wet asphalt. The invisible gust itself is rendered as a series of bold flat curved wave-lines radiating from one side and pressing into the wheel, like visible pressure ripples bending the spokes' shadow. The wheel and rim are rendered in flat weathered teal with one dark shading tone, the tire and skid mark in flat charcoal gray. The wheel sits at the exact center of the frame, tilted off-axis. In the mid-ground behind it, the jagged black silhouette of a wide-open storm-lashed road with leaning roadside grass looms, rendered as a pure flat black silhouette with no outline. In the far background, a heavy typhoon sky roils with a limited charcoal-to-teal tonal gradient and coarse halftone grain, tiny scattered dot texture like static. Bold, consistent black ink outlines on the wheel and any solid-form elements; the wind-wave lines remain outline-free flat light strokes. Overall high-contrast, screen-print/riso-print poster texture with rough grain and slight ink bleed throughout, on a solid charcoal black background. Vivid saturated color contrast: dusty teal and slate gray dominate, with one small acid-yellow accent glowing on the wheel's spoke reflector caught mid-blur. Layered composition: distant stormy open-road sky, mid-ground silhouetted leaning roadside grass, close-up skidding wheel and wind-wave lines in the foreground — everything centered in the middle of the frame, well clear of the four corners. Strictly avoid: smooth airbrushed digital gradients, photorealistic texture, glossy highlights, soft cel-shading, any circular badge border or ring frame outline, any text, lettering, words, typography, logos, watermarks, or signatures, and any purple/violet/lavender hues. Full-bleed square illustration filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no border, no frame.
